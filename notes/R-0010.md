## Findings

This is the release gate for articles/the-lead-was-a-few-libraries.md, its figure, its short form, the front page and the record studies/LH010/, with the LH010 lines in AGENTS.md and programme.md. The reviewer read AGENTS.md and R-0009, then the record, its brief and sources and the tables in data/, then the piece, the short form, the front page and the figure's text sentence by sentence against the record, and went to the primary sources as the dispatch asked. Every pooled rate in data/hazard_by_class.csv, every library rate, median, permutation p and leave-one-library-out range in library_rates.csv and library_unit.csv, the shares before the advisory and nltk's weight were recomputed from data/lags.csv with the reviewer's own short Python, and analyse.py was run on a scratch copy of the study and its outputs compared byte for byte. Nine fixed releases' notes were re-read at their tags from fresh clones (mako 1.3.11, uv 0.11.6, authlib 1.7.1, mistune 3.2.1, idna 3.15, aiohttp 3.13.4, tornado 6.5.5, python-dotenv 1.2.2, fastmcp 3.2.0), with the tag's type, its commit time and its annotation, and fastmcp's changelog history was walked by hand; nine releases' upload times and descriptions were read from PyPI; eleven libraries' events were re-derived from OSV and PyPI by LH008's rules 2 to 4; fifteen moves in fourteen dependents' repositories were re-read with a detector the reviewer wrote without reading collect.py's; and the figure was rendered with cairosvg (its CSS variables substituted, since cairosvg does not resolve them) and looked at.

The verdict is that the piece, the short form, the figure and the front page can be released after the changes below, which the reviewer made, and that the record is now version 0.2. Everything the record tabulates reproduced: every rate, median, range and count, the nine entries' words and classes, the eleven events, the fifteen moves to the second. What had to change was of two kinds. First, the record's rule for the advisory has a gap that the record did not see: an earlier GitHub-reviewed advisory naming the same fix, published before the window or before the fix, is not the event's advisory, and for three libraries with pairs (nltk, tornado, pip) such an advisory existed, so that nltk's 691 repository-days "before the advisory" and four of tornado's five moves came after a public advisory for the same release. The piece's opening said GitHub published tornado's advisory 23 days after the fix, when it had published two others for the release within two days. The reviewer computed the sensitivity with the earliest public advisory as the advisory time and put it in the record, the piece, the short form, the front page, AGENTS.md and programme.md. Second, the record's Answer said the lead "mostly goes away" and the piece's title that "the earlier lead came from a few libraries", when the pooled lead of about 1.5 times survives leaving out any one library and, without the three quickest announced libraries, is still 1.65 against 1.57; those claims were replaced by what the tables show. Smaller things: a count in the record's prose that its own table contradicts (221 reused packages, where reused_screen.csv has 201), a misquotation of python-dotenv's notes in the piece ("in some cases" for "in some situations"), the process timings, and the nltk and pip security-worded moves, whose messages and OSV say more than the record did.

### The writer's points, and what else was found

1. **Must fix, fixed. Advisories the window rule does not see.** For every one of the 43 events the reviewer looked in data/osv_advisories.csv for a GitHub-reviewed, unwithdrawn GHSA record naming the event's fixed release and published before the event's advisory.
