## Dependency and acceptance rules

Every dependency is all-of and requires `done`. `running`, `missed`, `cancelled`, a date arriving or a file existing does not clear an edge. Active tasks link retained instructions and state what their completion must establish. A completion reference must name the actual output or decision, not merely repeat the brief.

An evidence-batch or collection-closure gate is a decision about the scoped attempt history and the brief's stop, not an assertion that every observation succeeded. Its acceptance requires reconciling every scoped row to a recorded outcome, retaining actual attempts, unread sources and gaps, and establishing the applicable stop. A read cannot be declared missed before its permitted window expires or cancelled without a brief-supported reason. No closure authorises backfilling or extending collection. Such a gate can legitimately complete after missed/cancelled reads and permit an honest incomplete answer. It has no successful-observation AND chain that would prevent that answer forever.

Conditional early stops are explicit manual blocks, assessed at each read and the hard cutoff. The blocked stop tasks for LH030, LH036, LH040, LH049, LH050 and LH051 require the named evidence or fixed end, then a recorded unblock/closure decision. Their deadlines still show the cutoff; a block never extends collection. LH030 additionally requires the release applicability decision before reading release contents and the qualifying scheduled read at least two days after the release. LH036 preserves its KEV/14-day or January stop. LH050 preserves its earlier resource/access stops and 24 December hard end.

For checks, `done` means that named check is cleared for the actual retained version. A reviewer producing comments alone does not complete a check. Must-fix findings leave the task blocked while bounded revision work resolves them; revision work is owned by the session resolving the review and does not depend on the review being marked done. Then the same check can clear. Reader review follows evidence review; one fresh recheck reads the review resolution. A changed draft cannot inherit clearance from another version. Publication checks the actual notes and exact version as well as the graph. Historical generic review rows are used only for the retained snapshot their notes actually checked; no new approval is claimed here.
