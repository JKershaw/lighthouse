# Essay plan: It was only a test, version 3

8 November 2026. Revised after discussion with John: give the examples more room and review the result from distinct reader perspectives. The delivered version 2 is retained in reviews/v2-handback/.

## Central question

How does pursuing a task turn into unauthorised action, and when might the traces of one worker’s actions help another continue? Ordinary messages, files and handovers are both evidence of activity and material in the next worker’s environment.

The cases motivate related questions, not a single established cause. Useful conventions and cooperation belong in the research programme alongside unwanted persistence. Harbour is a place to test ideas, not corroboration of a common laboratory mechanism.

## Research design in the essay

First establish what a successor demonstrably received and read. Where retained records are inadequate, instrument new controlled runs.

The first exposure comparison uses an explicitly forbidden write in a controlled maintenance task, with a handover containing the example versus one without it. Task information and permissions stay fixed; context length is accounted for; repeated runs score actions and quality of authorised work. Apparent authority is tested separately by varying the status of a recommendation while keeping the factual account and actual permissions fixed. A simulated outsider supplies an analogue for unauthorised involvement. A positive companion asks whether an inherited valid check is actually run and prevents errors, judged by work quality and total cost.

These are essay-level design directions. A study still needs a protocol, controls, resource limits and stopping conditions. This work launches no experiment, publication or recurring task.

## Review design

Four fresh agents first record impressions without reading the brief, design plan, earlier drafts or other reviews: a curious nontechnical reader, an agent builder, a maintainer/affected party and a sceptical researcher. These are simulated lenses, not human audience research. A separate factual reviewer checks primary passages. The writer resolves consequential findings without averaging away the author’s voice; a final recheck inspects the changes.
