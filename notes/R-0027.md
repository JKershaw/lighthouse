# R-0027: reader-and-inference review of LH017's pieces

Reviewer: a subagent on Fable 5.1, 30 September 2026.

Read for this stage: the full piece, its figure (rendered), the short form and the README's "Current focus" section, and nothing else.

## What a reader would believe, written before reading the record

Said back: a Python project holding an older library version in a pin or lockfile nearly always has test or build jobs that install it from the pinned file. Of 469 moments across 332 projects, 395 had workflows; where the rules could decide, some job installed from the pinned file in 282 of 291, 97 per cent. Where a lockfile held the version, a job read the lockfile itself in 164 of 188, against 45 of 90 container recipes, though 83 of those only through commands that may rewrite it.

Observed: GitHub Actions and GitLab workflow files at the last commit before each release, classed by rules fixed beforehand, partly by hand, and checked blind on 40 jobs. Explained: recipes and tests part because of the install commands each uses; the lockfile figure is soft because loose uv commands can re-lock. Caused: the pinned file is what tests would install at every change, and the reader is offered, though explicitly unmeasured, a road from pins, through installers that flag automation, to the older versions in PyPI's download log.

Limits noticed: no run was read, so nothing shows what ran, how often or what was fetched, and many restore caches; any job counts; whether a job installs the library at all was not resolved; the projects came from earlier readings, not a sample; 50 were undecided, near a quarter among lockfile-only projects; units slip between projects and moments; the strict share's denominator shifts from 188 to 161 unexplained; and the bbot opening never says what its container installed.

## Material claim problems

1. **The comparison with the recipes mixes two pair rules.** LH015 calls a pair "from the pinned file" only when every build that installs the requirements is class 1; LH017 calls it "reads the pin" when any job is (brief, choice 4). The figure puts both under one heading: "Some build installs the version from the pin or lockfile: container recipes 129, 38 and 68 of 235; CI workflows 282, 9 and 50 of 341" (articles/tests-read-the-lockfile.md, line 28, and the SVG's subheading), and the investigation page says "The same projects' automated test and build workflows read the pin far more often" (investigations/software-updates.md, line 58). Evidence: under LH015's rule CI is 231 of 302, 76.5 per cent (data/sensitivities.csv, "P2, LH015's pair rule"), against the recipes' 129 of 167, 77.2 per cent; under LH017's rule applied to LH015's retained build classes, the recipes are 164 of 194, 84.5 per cent, since 30 of LH015's 68 undetermined pairs have a build that installs from the pin (studies/LH017/review/r0027_rules.txt). For the lock the gap survives but shrinks: recipes 71 of 113, 63 per cent, under LH017's rule, or CI 164 of 207, 79 per cent, under LH015's, against the pieces' 45 of 90 and 164 of 188; on the 65 pairs both studies decide, recipes read it in 40 and CI in 58. Resolution: draw the recipe bars under the CI bars' rule, or label each pair's rule; add one sentence in "Almost all kept tests": under the rule the recipes were read by, where one job installing without the pin counts against the project, the tests' share is 231 of 302, 77 per cent, the recipes' own; give the lockfile comparison on the 65 pairs decided in both; and on the investigation page keep "far more often" for the lockfile alone.

2. **"Projects" counts moments.** "some job installed the library from the pinned file for 282 of 291 projects at those moments" (article, line 2; short form, line 12; README, line 13). Evidence: the 291 decided pairs lie in 211 repositories, 203 of which read the pin at every decided moment (r0027_rules.txt; measures.csv, repository view 96.4 per cent). Resolution: "at 282 of the 291 moments", with "in 211 projects, 203 of them at every moment we could decide" once.

3. **The short form states a run.** "So the versions held in pins are installed again at each test run" (articles/short/tests-read-the-lockfile.md, line 14). The record: "A workflow is a recipe for a run, not a record of one" (LH017.md, "What the classes mean"), and 125 of the 282 restore a cache. The lightest of the three, since the next sentence corrects it. Resolution: "are what each test run is told to install".
