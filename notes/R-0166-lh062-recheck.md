## Changes made in studies/LH062/LH062.md

1. In short. Old: "keys found working on websites in September 2025 had been visible". New: "keys found on websites in September 2025 and confirmed working had been visible". Why: §4.1 validates "after the initial offline detection phase" and the text read doesn't date the checks to September.
2. In short. Old: "and no two documents counted the same thing, so each figure". New: "and no two of the documents read counted the same thing, so each figure". Why: the absolute's window.
3. Answer, first bullet. Old: "Credentials found exposed and valid on websites' landing pages in September 2025 had been public". New: "Credentials found exposed on websites' landing pages in September 2025 and confirmed valid had been public". Same reason as 1.
4. Answer, first bullet. Old: "known case). Because the sample is credentials still exposed and working at the end of the window, it holds". New: "known case). In our reading of the design (interpretation), because the sample is credentials still exposed at the end of the window and confirmed working, it holds". Why: the selection argument is Lighthouse's reading inside a bullet headed "observations"; it reads the design, not outcomes, so it isn't post hoc.
5. Answer, after-notification bullet. Old: "(the authors' denominator, 1,668 plus "the failed 767 websites")". New: "(the denominator their counts imply, 1,668 plus "the failed 767 websites")". Findings, A07 paragraph. Old: "the authors' denominator is 2,435 websites, 1,668 plus "the failed 767"". New: "the denominator their counts imply is 2,435 websites, 1,668 plus "the failed 767"". Why: the authors state 1,668, 767 and 68.5 per cent, and 2,435 *notifications* (2,279 first-party, 156 third-party), but never 2,435 websites; the sum is Lighthouse's.
6. Limits, A07's average. Old: "drawn from credentials found exposed and valid at the end of its window (September 2025)". New: "drawn from credentials found exposed at the end of its window (September 2025) and confirmed valid".
