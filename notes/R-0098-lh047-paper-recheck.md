## Findings

### Must-fix

1. **"14 of them only because the rule covers that crawler's name"** (body, paragraph beginning "And that same evening"). Only 9 of the 19 are disallowed by the ClaudeBot group alone. The 14 are those no group for Anthropic's user-triggered fetcher or agents rules out; 5 of them are also ruled out by groups naming Claude-SearchBot, anthropic-ai or Claude-Web. Replace the sentence's second half. Exact replacement for

   > Applied afterwards to the same requests, that rule catches 19 more, all of them in groups that name ClaudeBot, Anthropic's training crawler, and 14 of them only because the rule covers that crawler's name: 191 in all.

   with

   > Applied afterwards to the same requests, that rule catches 19 more, all of them in groups that name ClaudeBot, Anthropic's training crawler: 191 in all. 14 of the 19 break no group addressed to the names Anthropic gives the fetcher that reads a page when a person asks, or its agents.

   (Or, keeping the ClaudeBot framing: "...all of them in groups that name ClaudeBot, Anthropic's training crawler, and 9 of them only because the rule covers that crawler's name: 191 in all.") The first ties to the closing question about crawler and fetcher tokens; the second is the literal count. The figure, desc and alt text don't state the 14 and need no change.

### Should-fix (not blocking)

2. **The dateline still says "Draft, not yet reviewed"** in both the article (line 14) and the short form, and the short form's colophon says "reviews to come", while the article's colophon now says its evidence review has been done and resolved. Suggest "*15 November 2026 · Lighthouse · Draft, not yet released*" in both, and in the short form's colophon "its evidence review's fixes were made on 19 November 2026; its reader review is to come" (R-0082 reviewed the short form too).
3. **"robots.txt rules ruled out"** (legend, desc, alt text) repeats a word and reads stiffly. "12 other requests robots.txt rules disallowed, 5 because the file couldn't be reached" says the same; the reader review can weigh it.
