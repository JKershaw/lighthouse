# Microsoft 365 outage report watch: the substantive-report gate, 22 November 2026

**In short.** The watch was waiting for a readable copy of Microsoft's full post-incident report on the outage of 12 and 13 November, to compare with its line that the outage was "not related to cyber or AI / agents". On 22 November the Watch run read a 15-page report in Microsoft's form and wording, dated 20 November, from a consultancy's copy, since the original sits in an admin centre Lighthouse doesn't enter. We decide that this copy clears the gate, with its provenance stated wherever it is used: the comparison now starts and the remaining daily availability checks stop.

Decided by the Research run of 22 November 2026 (run `2026-11-22T151100Z-anthropic-research`, Claude Code on Anthropic's cloud), from the retained readings of 21 and 22 November. No source was read for this decision.

## The criteria

Row MS365-substantive-report asks for "an accessible substantive Microsoft incident report under the fixed watch criteria (not a restored/status notice)", naming the exact report, date, access provenance and basis. The watch (notes/microsoft-outage-report-watch.md, rules 1 and 4) prefers Microsoft-authored material and asks that tenant copies and journalism be told apart from it; its own baseline is a tenant's published copy. It forbids tenant login and private admin-centre access, so the original in the admin centre is out of reach by design.

## The report and its basis

- **Report:** "Post Incident Report For Microsoft 365", Report Date: November 20, 2026, incident MO1550821 (and EX1550543), 15 pages, with Microsoft's copyright and disclaimer pages.
- **Access provenance:** https://www.365fanatic.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/postincidentreport-mo1550821.pdf, read 22 November 2026 14:47:21 UTC, HTTP 200, 252,860 bytes, SHA-256 d77e84e6d58d7eeeac7cb6416af774a399b99b2f3e782bd6928361940136e1b2, allowed by robots.txt; body not kept (Microsoft's notice forbids storage in a retrieval system). The file's metadata says it was made with Microsoft Word on 21 November 2026 07:53 UTC, so it is a third party's copy or re-export, not a file served by Microsoft ([reading of 22 November, addendum](readings/2026-11-22.md)). Read as a fifth document with the keeper's permission given in that session.
- **Why it is taken as the report:** it is substantive (trigger, three operational conditions, why testing missed the defect, a list of repair items), not a status notice; its date and identifiers match Microsoft's own posts, which gained "A post-incident report has been published." at 01:04 UTC on 21 November in two independent public copies of the posts, a mirror and a tenant's admin-console extract ([reading of 21 November](readings/2026-11-21.md)); and the consultancy's summary page of 21 November describes the same report.
- **What it cannot establish:** that the copy matches the admin-centre original word for word. No public source lets us check that, and the watch's rules forbid the route that would. So every use of it says it is a third party's copy of a document presented as Microsoft's, not Microsoft's own publication of it.

## Decision and what follows

The gate is cleared on this basis (row MS365-substantive-report done, referencing this note). Following watch rule 5: MS365-report-compare is opened; the availability rows for 23 November to 11 December are cancelled, their reason being that the report was collected and comparison work has started; MS365-report-close stays, so the watch still closes formally by 12 December. The comparison follows the watch's fixed categories (confirm, qualify, contradict, leave unaddressed, non-comparable), makes no causal inference from what the report doesn't mention, and goes through the evidence review, reader-and-inference review and recheck before any public line.

A routine choice noted here: the comparison may read the same PDF once more, as a one-off read for analysis, keeping only the hash, the facts used and short quotations, and checking the hash against the one above; a different hash is reported, not explained away.
