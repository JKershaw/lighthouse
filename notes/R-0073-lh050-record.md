Not-established is used where a readable source is silent. Not-applicable is declined, with a reason. Conflicting is used once. 6. **Absolutes against the window.** "No source read says...", "No delivery ... is established", "No cell ... is supported" are each scoped to sources read on 13 November 2026 or to the matrix. One open-ended present tense was found in the Answer and fixed (see changes).

8. **Harm and use of force.** The record makes no claim of harm or of use of force in Lighthouse's voice. Statements about autonomy in combat are attributed to the reporting outlet and, where given, to the chief executive. The ministry's figures are attributed as its own counts, and their silence on armament and civilians is recorded rather than read as a negative. The Next Web's lines on drone strikes and Senator Warren's allegation are not used.

## What was found

No must-fix findings. Minor findings, each fixed in place:

- The Answer labelled the matrix totals "(observation, from the matrix)". They are counts of this record's codings, so they are derived (and rest on interpretation-labelled cells).
- The Answer said one Changi robot was "in operational use since April 2023" and one "on trial since July 2025". As written, that reads as continuing to November 2026. The sources are dated June 2023 and July 2025, and the matrix notes later status as not established. The sentence now dates each to its source.
- C033 and the deployment-evidence cell gave "front-line US deployment within 12 to 18 months" (The Next Web) and cited eWeek's read alongside it. eWeek's version is weaker: "hopes to begin testing its robots with the US military within the next 12 to 18 months". The brief asks for evidence against expansion to be kept as carefully as evidence for it, so both are now given. The claims ledger (claims.csv C033) cites only The Next Web and was left as it is.
- HTX says the public was "largely unresponsive" to the "Excuse me" message and "didn't seem to notice it". The record and matrix said "largely ignored", which implies intent. Changed.
- TechCentral's article ends with a "Business AM" credit and may be a syndicated copy. This is added to the Limits line on source families. It strengthens the record's existing point that repetition adds no corroboration.

Noted, not changed: the record's "mast camera" glosses SPF's "extendable mast with 360-degree field of view". The source says "field of view", not "camera". This is a fair reading, but a reader-reviewer may prefer the source's words. The GIBSON intervention cell counts as supported a mobility takeover, not a force intervention. The record says so in the cell and the text, and readers of the totals should keep it in mind.
