1. `PHP vulnerability exploited in the wild`: 10 results, all older PHP flaws (CVE-2024-4577, CVE-2019-11043, CVE-2012-1823, CVE-2012-2376). The tool says nothing newer than 2025 surfaced. No lead.
2. `PHP2Shell`: 9 results. Tool summary: the flaws are CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, disclosed about 21 October 2026, fixed in PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35, each scored 9.2 on CVSS v4.0 (the tool notes that one French source says some outlets reported 10); sources differ on affected versions (InvGate "before 8.5", a Spanish outlet back to 5.4). The tool says Dark Reading reports a broken embargo, a circulating proof-of-concept tool and exploitation attempts. Lead, unread: https://www.darkreading.com/application-security/php2shell-bugs-red-alert-security-industry
3. `PHP session upload progress vulnerability CVE-2026-115560`: 9 results. Tool summary: nothing for CVE-2026-115560 itself, which the tool calls unverified; every relevant result concerns CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. Mend.io is said to describe operations run before the next request is initialised (INI diagnostics, session upload progress) using a stale pointer; several sources are said to suggest `session.upload_progress.enabled = 0`, and one researcher is said to say it blocks only one of the two bugs. Unread: https://lilting.ch/en/articles/php2shell-session-upload-progress-uaf
4. `PHP vulnerability websites taken offline`: 9 results. Tool summary: Reuters (via The Star) is said to report government, school and hospital sites in Europe offline on 21 October, CERT-EU advice to consider taking sites down if they could not patch, Germany's ITZBund calling its move preventive; Cloudflare is said to report early attacks and Australia's agency to say it knew of no active exploitation. No primary source linked by the tool.
5. `PHP vulnerability AI discovered Gemini OpenAI Wiz`: 10 results. Tool summary: Wiz says it used Wiz Atlas with Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol; OpenAI says Calif found one flaw with GPT-5.6 Sol under Patch the Planet, with Wiz independently reporting both; Calif is said to have said it found the flaws with an LLM on 1 August. The tool also mentions a secondhand report that the Wall Street Journal described the flaw as found with GPT-5.6 Sol in August, and a report that the White House was made aware on a Friday. The tool says the sources disagree and the accounts are mostly secondary. Unread: https://ppc.land/openai-says-gpt-5-6-sol-found-a-php-flaw-before-attackers-did/
6. `PHP vulnerability affected products vendors patch`: 9 results, 7 of them old advisories. Tool summary: ITPro is said to relay that PHP may be embedded in third-party products, that PHP 8.1 and earlier get no regular security releases while the PHP security team published limited source patches for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1, and CERT-EU's "PHP 5 onwards" scope. No vendor-by-vendor list found.
