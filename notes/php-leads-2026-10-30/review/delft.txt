 PHP2Shell in the wild
 Exploitation of CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 (PHP2Shell, the PHP session.upload_progress use-after-free) as seen by the honeypots and network telescope of the Delft Threat Intelligence Lab since 20 October 2026. Our own test traffic is left out and our sensor addresses are replaced by <sensor>. All times are UTC. The data is refreshed every 5 minutes.
