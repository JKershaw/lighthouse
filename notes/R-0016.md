## Findings

This is the release gate for LH012 and the piece it earned. The reviewer read AGENTS.md, the brief, its three amendments, the record, its sources, the piece, its short form and the two figure scripts, and the surface changes in 08c505b; checked the brief's hash against the one posted on issue #14 before any count was read; reran the replay; recomputed every headline figure from the retained files with its own code; re-read a sample of the counts and metadata from ClickPy and PyPI; read the analysis code at the five points the research agent asked a reviewer to check first; rendered both figures in light and dark; and traced every figure in the piece and short form to a table. Every figure in the record's Answer stands, and every live re-read agreed with the retained file. What had to be fixed was one figure in a secondary measure and three sentences in the piece: the wheel-exclusion test had counted litellm's older `py3-none-any` wheels as excluded because their platform could not be matched, where the platform is simply not known (58.1 per cent, now set apart as not testable); the piece said older by number was older by date "for every library but one" where the writer's own check shows two; its summary said three explanations were tested where the third was described, not counted; and its alt text said red was most of the bar for numpy where it is most of the older part. The verdict is that the piece can be released, with the record at version 0.2.

The reviewer's calculations are kept in studies/LH012/review/: review.py and review.txt (the older share and its descriptors, P and its classes with intervals, the week-end admission table against each day's, B with its guard for all 37 projects, the frozen-list trace under amendment 3.1 and under the brief's literal wording, the residual, the file classes with `any` wheels set apart, the first-day test, the "two ways" table and the read logs, all from data/ without calling analyse.py), and requery.py and requery.txt (the live re-reads, with each query's time, SQL and rows read).

### Must fix, fixed

1. **Wheels tagged `any` counted as wheel-excluded** (record, Findings; scripts/analyse.py; data/analysis/files.csv; amendments.md). litellm's older downloads were reported as 58.1 per cent wheel-excluded because its older `py3-none-any` wheels could not be matched to its newer platform-tagged wheels, though such a download names no platform to test. By the brief's rule that unknown stays unknown, analyse.py now sets them apart as "wheel tagged any, platform not known"; the replay regenerated files.csv (litellm 0.0 per cent excluded and 58.1 in the new class; aiohttp 33.5 to 33.4; nothing else changes). Recorded as amendment 4, labelled post hoc, and in Corrections; the record goes to version 0.2, and the piece, short form and programme.md cite it. The measure never entered P, so no classification changes.
2. **"For every library but one, older by number was also older by date"** (piece, "What stays behind"). The driver's own check gives two: grpcio-status had 1.5 per cent of its older downloads on 1.82.2, a patch uploaded after the reference. Now "For 35 of the 37 libraries ... grpcio-status had 1.5 per cent of its older downloads on a later patch to an earlier line. The exception that matters is pydantic-core".
3. **"We tested three explanations, fixed before reading"** (piece, summary). The third, frozen lists, was described and not counted, as the brief said in advance and the body says. Now "We fixed three explanations before reading and tested the two the log can count."
4. **The alt text** (piece). "Red makes up most of the bar only for aiobotocore, boto3 and numpy" is false for numpy, where red is 46 per cent of the bar; now "most of the older part of the bar", and "a large part of it" for the four named after.
