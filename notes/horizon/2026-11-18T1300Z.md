### What each scout found

**1. AI activity online.** Google's threat intelligence group (16 November) reports, for the first time, actors using an LLM framework (`exploit_agent`) to go from patch differences to working exploits "without a human in the loop", names ARTEX among five frameworks that "automate discrete phases", and reports LLMjacking and resold keys; it describes no self-propagation. Microsoft's index shows the ARTEX posts of 5 and 6 November and two lab posts. Cloudflare's 5 November paper, via ppc.land, says "the Site bears the primary cost" of agents. No new agent incident on a public site since Epoch's of 13 November.

**2. Security.** KEV is at 2026.11.17 with one addition since 13 November (Puppet Enterprise, due in 14 days). The Google report again. Forescout (12 November): 66 new TCP/IP stack flaws from AI-assisted research, 54 reported, no CVEs yet. PaperCut exploited within a day of disclosure. CERT-EU's OpenJDK advisory (18 November). Project Zero quiet since 13 November; nothing AI-found in oss-security's titles.

**3. Supply chain.** StepSecurity, Socket and Aikido report two releases of `agent-framework` on PyPI (17 November, 20:35 and 21:08 UTC), each quarantined within about 31 and 8 minutes, with a `.pth` stealer aimed at AI and cloud keys; the second came 2 minutes 23 seconds after the first was quarantined (P-0040). Optimizely confirmed the flaw behind the scripts incident (L-0146). PyPI, Hugging Face and GitHub's changelog quiet.

**4. Infrastructure.** Cloudflare Radar's baseline shows nothing new since 3 November. The rotation I33 to I40 found no change of coverage, access or method; M-Lab's blog has had nothing since 18 August, MAWILab ended in December 2024, Exploit-DB's search-dork list now includes Bing and GitHub. GreyNoise's AI Cyber Index and live exploitation reports. Microsoft's final outage review is reported as expected on 20 November.

**5. Research.** arXiv listings of 17 and 18 November (partly covered; listed in the log): exploitation timelines and their bad dates (2611.22479), the GitHub Actions backporting gap (2611.22519), agents stopping early (2611.22888), measurement choices in studies of agent-written code (2611.21784); a June paper on agent-driven worms seen only in a search summary (2606.03811). Nothing on self-propagation newer than 17 November.

**6. Policy and physical authority.** The CCW Review Conference, 16 to 20 November, has no outcome yet: at least 50 states want binding rules on a schedule, the US proposes five years of non-binding guidelines (NPR, 16 November). No revised Directive 3000.09. Web Bot Auth's milestones have no documents and IETF 127 material isn't posted. Nothing new from the EU AI Office, CAISI, the UK AI Security Institute or the courts.

**7. Wildcard.** The Washington Sun's anonymous sources say patching for model-found flaws is behind the November outages; every on-record statement it quotes gives another cause (L-0150). Quad9 leaves France on 24 November under a court penalty (L-0151). Codeberg's domain was suspended by its registrar for some hours on 18 November (L-0152). No armed-robot item dated November 2026 in seven languages; an older gap: remotely operated guns on cranes in Gaza reported firing on civilians since June (L-0153). Ukraine's October count of ground-robot missions (L-0154). A dpa report adds to L-0112 (Phantasialand): the email-loss account is the park's, via WDR, and the fire brigade said nothing on cause.

**Gaps.** OpenAI's misuse index and Google's full index unread; GitHub's advisory database and crates.io's malware feed unread; no CCW outcome yet; nothing opened in Korean, Hebrew or Turkish, and nothing from Africa, South or Southeast Asia or Latin America, through a US-indexed search tool.
