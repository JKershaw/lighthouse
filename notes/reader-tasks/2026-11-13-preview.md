# Reader tasks and measurements on the preview

**In short.** The same three fresh reader agents and four tasks as [the baseline](2026-11-13-baseline.md), run on the step 3 preview (the new front page, This week page and investigation pages, built with a draft answer that has had no review). Finding what Lighthouse knows fell from three to five pages to one, and its ease rose from 2 to between 4 and 5; the other tasks held. The readers' new complaints were fixed in the preview before it went to the keeper, except those in the investigation pages' own text, listed below for the investigations' upkeep.

## What the readers found, and what was done

- **This week lost its links on its own page.** Fixed: items render from the repository root on every page.
- **"Where it stands: Reviewed" on the AI-cost investigation meant an essay.** Fixed: an investigation with no current account says "No reviewed finding yet; working records only" with the Provisional badge, and shows its newest essay beside that as reading.
- **The run log's internal language on the This week page** (codes, throttle, spend). Fixed: that page leaves out the run line and the keeper lines, which stay on the schedule.
- **Many labels with no key.** A one-line key to Reviewed, Provisional and Live is in every page's footer. Released, Revised and Developing remain in a piece's own date line.
- **Not fixed here, for the investigations' upkeep:** the AI-cost investigation page puts "Where the evidence stands" at the bottom of a long page, in long paragraphs mixing reviewed and unreviewed lines, and carries internal detail (a person's first name approving publication, an issue number, the throttle). A short In short opening and moving that section up would answer most of task 3's friction. This Week left out the map piece revised on 9 November and the essays, and "Being looked into" still names the AI-cost investigation as a proposal; both are editor's-visit matters.
- **Out of reach of templates:** findings' titles are long sentences and study titles are cryptic. Topic labels above titles help lists scan; titles change only through reviews.
