# Reader tasks and measurements, baseline

**In short.** Before any change for the reader's site (notes/reader-experience-plan-2026-11-13.md), three fresh agents with no context browsed the built site as of commit a142459 through a text-only view (scripts/reader_view.py), and the pages were measured at phone and desktop width. All three could find this week and the AI-cost investigation at once, but none could find what Lighthouse knows without assembling it from three to five pages, and all three met a reviewed figure on the front page without a status beside it.

## The four tasks

1. In two plain sentences, what does Lighthouse know so far, at its current certainty, and did the site say so clearly?
2. What happened this week (for the returning reader, since 6 November)?
3. Where would you go to learn what Lighthouse has found about what AI activity costs the shared internet and whether any of it spreads itself?
4. The figure "24.4 million" (PHP services) is somewhere on the site: is it reviewed, and how can you tell?

Task 1 pages: About, the programme (abandoned by two readers at 64 to 76 KB), the charter and the knowledge map; the clearest sentence ("No report read so far describes AI activity sustaining itself outside a lab") was found only later, on the AI-cost investigation page.

## What all three reported

- **No current answer.** The front page states the question but not where Lighthouse stands; the In focus lead (who watches each layer) is not about the central question. About and the charter say "we do not know" mid-paragraph, reading as mission text, not a dated state of knowledge.
- **Status is inconsistent.** A badge in some places, a phrase mid-sentence in others, nothing in the front page's background text, where "24.4 million" sits beside a link to the unreviewed daily log.
- **Internal language on reader surfaces.** Study and proposal codes, "keeper", "throttle", "driver", spend figures, "record 0.3", "amendment 1", mostly from the Now box's Latest run line.
- **Repetition and length.** The PHP emergency, the observations list, About and the investigations each appear two or three times on the front page; the programme and releases pages are very long with no summary at the top.
- **The AI-cost investigation** is easy to find but long, mixes reviewed and unreviewed lines flagged only within each line, and lists two essays as its only pieces.
- Smaller: This week mixes Reported and Lighthouse items with the caveat at the end; the knowledge map says "three questions" and lists four; the same title appears in both "Being looked into" and "Waiting for the keeper".
