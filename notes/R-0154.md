## Findings

1. **Wrong figure, carried into five places (corrected).** R-0153 wrote "Even if every pull request event the files hold were a probe opening, they couldn't hold most of Aikido's count". But 41,014 of 61,839 is 66 per cent, which is most. The resolution repeated this as: "so they could never have held most of it" (In short), "the share was capped well below the brief's 50 per cent" (Answer), "The pre-set reading could not have come out 'most' for Aikido's window" (measure 3), and "the files' volume caps any campaign's share" (P-0052). It also stated the new reading more surely than its evidence: "The low share says more about the files than about the campaign" (In short), "the share mainly measures how little of GitHub's activity the hourly files hold" (measure 3), and "a share capped mostly by how few pull request events the files hold" (programme.md). What the comparison does show is that the files are at most a partial record of GitHub's pull request activity, since in Aikido's window they hold fewer of every kind than its probe count alone. A cap of 66 per cent doesn't explain a share of 0.96 per cent, and how much of the share the files' volume explains isn't measured.
2. **Absolute without support (corrected).** "with about 1 in 100 probes held, few closing events would appear in the files whatever happened on GitHub". If most probes had closed and closings were held at the openings' rate, hundreds would appear. The supportable claim is that the share of later closings held isn't measured, so the count can't be scaled up.
3. **Stale against the changed header (corrected).** The Answer's "Derived measurement, unreviewed", Limits' "**Unreviewed.** No second agent has reproduced any figure yet" and Next's first item (reviews still to come) contradicted version 1.2's header and Corrections. The In short's "a recheck comes next" was also updated.
4. **Post hoc labels.** Present at each new reading, with "one comparison". The In short's new sentence lacked the label; it now has "a check made after the counts were seen". The round names P-0052 as the test, now also beside the reading in the Answer and measure 3.
5. **Checked, no change.** "weak evidence that new probes stopped" is a hedge, not an overclaim. Note, though, that at about 1 in 100 a trickle of a few probes an hour sustained over the two weeks to 26 November would leave tens of held openings, so the absence is weak evidence only about the hours just after 12 November. I left this because it understates rather than overstates. The StepSecurity "over a third" sentence, the deletion days, the onset parenthesis and the Limits "No cause" interpretation (still "rules out only LH046's gap", now labelled) all match the data. R-0151's N2 (model disclosure wording) wasn't touched by the resolutions and I didn't reopen it.
