## site/learned.md

- **Headline, "A month after two fixes, most of the rise came in two weeks".** Fair to the piece. Its title says "both counts had done most of their rising within two weeks", and its close says "most of what moved, moved in the first fortnight". The headline doesn't mention the old layer, but the body does.
- **Sentence 1, "A month after PHP's and Node.js's security fixes of 21 October 2026, both counts we followed had done most of their rising in the first two weeks."** In the piece's summary and text ("both counts we followed had done most of their rising within two weeks"; "Nearly all of that came in the first two weeks").
