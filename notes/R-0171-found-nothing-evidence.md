## Findings

### Must fix

**M1. The summary dates the count to the wrong day and widens "pip" to "newer installers".** Passage: "On 17 November 2026, a public copy of the Python package index's download log held 79,782,285 rows for one popular library, and not one was a request for the small metadata file that newer installers read." The rows are for downloads on 17 November, counted on 19 November (LH013 header; read log 14:28:30 UTC); a summary is read without the body. The record documents pip from 23.2 reading metadata files, not installers in general. Fix (applied): "A public copy of the Python package index's download log holds 79,782,285 rows for one popular library's downloads on 17 November 2026, and when we counted them two days later not one was a request for the small metadata file that newer versions of pip read."

**M2. The after-the-fact choice of cases isn't disclosed in the body.** The seven cases and their kinds were picked after every record's outcome was known (working note, "Post hoc grouping"). The colophon says so; the prose doesn't, and the piece's "we found each was ..." reads as a finding across cases. AGENTS.md asks that a reading chosen after outcomes were seen be labelled in the piece, and that cases chosen for their outcome be said to be so. Fix (applied): after "None was a no." in the idea paragraph, "We picked these seven, and sorted them into those kinds, after we knew how each study had come out, so they're illustrations, not a sample of everything we've looked for."; the summary adds "chosen after we knew how each study ended"; the Methods line says no study tests the grouping.

**M3. "In every version we read" has no window and reaches past the reviewed record.** Passage: "In every version we read, the latest of them dated 13 November, the flaw wasn't there." Versions 2026.11.18, 2026.11.23 and 2026.11.25 were also read (19, 23 and 26 November; check_output.txt), in reads no review has covered. They agree, but the piece rests on the record reviewed to 16 November and shouldn't imply that those were the only versions read. Fix (applied): "In every version we read from 1 to 16 November, the latest of them dated 13 November, the flaw wasn't there." Short form likewise: "in the versions we read to 16 November".

**M4. "Each release entry says 'This is a security release'" is wrong for the 23 November read.** Passage: "As we read the page on 19 and 23 November, each release entry says "This is a security release", and none names the flaw." At 23 November the page holds five entries dated 19 Nov 2026; the fifth, "PHP 8.6.0 Released!", is a feature release that doesn't say so (check_output.txt). It names no CVE either, so "none names the flaw" holds. (LH030's record says "The four entries dated 19 Nov 2026 have the same visible text at both reads", which is true of the four security releases but misses the fifth; for the record's owner.) Fix (applied): "The next releases, for four branches of PHP, came on 19 November. As we read the page on 19 and 23 November, the entry for each of those four releases says "This is a security release", and none names the flaw."

**M5. The databases sentence is looser than the record.** Passage: "six of the seven we watched answered, at every read until PHP's releases of 19 November, that they held no record of the flaw." The record: "Six of the seven trackers answered at every read that they held no record until after PHP's releases were on its news page on 19 November (OSV at every read)". The read of 19 November 14:17 UTC came after the releases were on the page (Last-Modified 14:00 GMT) and still found no record at those six; five opened between that read and 23 November, and OSV never did; Ubuntu gave no answer at seven reads. "Until PHP's releases" invites the reading that records appeared with the releases. Fix (applied): "six of the seven we watched answered, whenever they answered, that they held no record of the flaw, up to and including our read of 19 November, taken after PHP's releases were on its news page. Five opened records by our next read, on 23 November; one still had none."

**M6. The piece implies the kernel announcement was once read.** Passage: "Whether the announcement changed after that is unknown to us." LH036: "The kernel CVE team's announcement: never read." The three earlier reads returned bot-check pages (derived files, `challenge_page: true`), so there is no "before" to compare. Fix (applied): "We never read the announcement itself, so what it says, and whether it changed, is unknown to us."

### Suggestions (all applied in 0.2 unless marked)

**S1. Title.** 0.1's title is long and its "seven times" reads as a count of all times. Applied: "Seven nothings we met in October and November 2026, and why none of them was a no" (window kept; "we met" leaves room for the ones not shown). Alternatives for the reader review: "Seven times we found nothing in October and November 2026, and what each could and couldn't say"; or, as the question, "When we found nothing in October and November 2026, what had we found?".

**S2. The file-name column limit.** Added to the opening's caveats: "or, less likely, the column holding file names might have been empty that day" (LH013 Limits of Stage A: "unlikely, not excluded").

**S3. Label the CISA reading.** "the catalogue records CISA's decision to list a flaw" became "as we read it, the catalogue records ...", since no CISA text on inclusion was read.

**S4. Scope of the model disclosure.** "this case and the next are about the flaw in Anthropic's post" became "this case, the next and the kernel announcement further down", since the walls and robots.txt sections concern the same flaw.

**S5. "Nearest" dated event.** LH038 names two nearest dated events, the reservation and the fix's author date, and uses neither as a bound. Now "The earliest dated event is ... reserved on 25 September, but a reservation isn't a report, and we haven't treated it, or the fix's author date, as one."

**S6. Whose announcement.** "the kernel's own announcement" became "the kernel security team's announcement" (LH036: the kernel CVE team's announcement on git.kernel.org).

**S7. Placeholders.** "they used placeholders instead" became "they used placeholders, or left them unnamed" (Anthropic's post leaves its package unnamed; LH043 Findings).

**S8. **S9. LH060 found two documents, not none.** Added "What the two report is in our record, but two can't describe a body of research." before "That isn't a finding ...". (The piece's "organisations" for the record's "operators" was left; the record's own question is about operators and its one size breakdown counts organisations.)

**S10. Colophon.** Version line and Reviews updated to 0.2, naming this review and the reader review still to come. The Sources line was checked against the read logs and needs no change.

**S11. Not applied, for the release round.** (a) The colophons name the run id; the released dates piece names the records and reviews in words instead.
