### The writer's open questions

1. **Must fix, fixed. The count of images read.** The Answer said "37 images had their installed `mcp` read and one was found to hold none", which a reader takes as 37 in all; the piece, short form and front page said "37 images of six projects" and then named jesse's `mcp`-less image among them. The programme paragraph's "the installed `mcp` in 37 images" was already right and is unchanged.

2. **Must fix, fixed. Header: "two older and one later bracketing image".** The images outside 20 March to 27 May are serena `dev` (11 December 2025) and octobot 3.0.0-beta1 (30 July 2026): one older, one later, as the piece's colophon already said.

3. **Should fix, fixed. The 8 April tag move is an inference.** The record labels it ("the tag's history is inference from the workflow"); the piece said "was published by moving a tag" and the short form "as agentcrew's was on 8 April". The evidence is strong (the workflow gives every CI build the `vX.Y.Z` tag, the CI build of 0.12.3 no longer carries it, the 8 April image does, has no labels and a +07:00 clock) but no registry shows the tag's history. One clause in each.

4. **Should fix, fixed. keboola "moved only when the pin did".** keboola has no public repository, so its pin commit is not seen. What is seen is that the image built at 12:50 on 17 April holds package version 1.56.2 with `mcp` 1.27.0, and that PyPI 1.56.2, published 2.7 days later, is the first release to require 1.27.0. The Answer now says the move with the pin is inferred from that, and the piece's own sentence already said it that way.

5. **Settled. jesse's 14 February pin against its `mcp`-less 28 March image.** From the clone: the commit `requirements.txt` gained `mcp==1.26.0` in (7300efb5, 14 February) is on master's first-parent chain, which is what scripts/collect_pins.py walks, and is an ancestor of the 2.1.3 commit (08b81849, 20 May). It is not an ancestor of the 28 March commits (cad0cfae, "Fix package build inputs", 15:04 UTC, eleven minutes before the 1.13.8 image was built and PyPI 1.13.8 published) nor of "Bump version to 1.13.8" (b64c867d, 16 March); at those commits `requirements.txt` has no `mcp` line, `setup.py` reads its requirements from that file, and the Dockerfile runs `pip3 install -r requirements.txt` then `pip3 install -e .`. So the 1.13.x images and releases came from another line of the repository than the one the pin table follows, and the record's three statements (a pin from 14 February, no `mcp` in the 28 March image, no PyPI requirement until 2.1.3) are all true of different lines. A Findings entry and a Limits entry now say so. Not checked: when that line was merged, and from which line 2.0.0 to 2.1.2 (which require no `mcp`) were built.

6. **Fixed. image_mcp.csv `method` "lockfile only".** The value meant "no dist-info seen; a requirements or lock file was seen", and in both rows the file had no `mcp` pin. Renamed in scripts/collect_images.py and in the two rows of the table; analyse.py does not test for the old string; the Method sentence names the old value.
