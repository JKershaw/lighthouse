**In short.** The first prototype put too much checking detail in the reader's path. At John's request, this iteration adds an overview of Lighthouse's three investigations and makes the existing trails and evidence separate levels. It also draws a larger documented history within the software-update investigation, without claiming a new research benefit.

## Reader design

1. Overview at /map/knowledge-lineage/: three questions, a legible larger research-history drawing, routes to the two detailed trails and the full catalogue. Status and consequential uncertainty remain visible; routine provenance is optional.
2. Thread at /map/knowledge-lineage/trails/: concise cards, with dates, authorship and pinpoints inside native disclosures. Evidence at /map/knowledge-lineage/evidence/ retains the original typed relationships, sample and qualifications.
3. Every view works with native links and without JavaScript. The drawing has phone and desktop layouts, directly linked records and a text equivalent. No zoom or hover is required to read a label. Large-scope coverage and original-source limitations have one clearly labelled explanation rather than a repeated caveat on every card.
