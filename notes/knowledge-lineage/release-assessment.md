**In short.** Lighthouse's existing work can now be explored through questions, a selected visual history and individual evidence-linked records. Every study in the declared catalogue has a checked explanation. This is useful navigation and provenance infrastructure; whether it improves readers' understanding or research decisions remains an open empirical question.

## What maintains itself

Each build discovers current records and explicitly released publications and regenerates both HTML and the downloadable catalogue. New records remain reachable even before an explanation is curated. A source hash change withholds its reviewed explanation until checked; changed evidence behind the selected history withholds that drawing. This conservative guard can also withhold text after harmless formatting changes.

Agents still need to check meaning, write short explanations and select useful histories. Do that within existing publication/correction reviews, checking only affected annotations and retaining their provenance. The [maintenance instructions](maintenance.md) separate this reviewed curation revision from the original experimental baseline, which stays immutable. No citation or similarity algorithm decides that one idea caused another.

## What should happen next

Keep this as a permanent navigation layer, then test it with an unfamiliar reader on a phone. Ask them to find a result, describe its population, follow a correction and identify an unresolved question. Retain wrong turns and misunderstandings; inspect assistive-technology behaviour too. Those observations should guide the next visual iteration.

For research usefulness, record whether a future investigation actually reuses earlier evidence, avoids duplicate work or changes its next question because of the map. Compare against the existing workflow before claiming a gain. The first experiment's equal retrieval scores do not warrant that claim.

The larger opportunity is separate inward and outward views over evidence-backed records: a system overview, selected explanatory histories and detailed source routes. Expand outward using one bounded, reviewed literature corpus and established scholarship. Keep citations, attributed influence, verified evidence and measured practical outcomes as distinct relationships, with gaps and conflicting accounts visible. Expand the visual projection when checked material warrants it, rather than trying to draw everything at once.
