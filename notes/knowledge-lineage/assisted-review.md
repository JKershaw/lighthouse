The graph itself states no measured deployment, influence or social-benefit edges.

As my proposed requirement, rather than an existing finding, uptake would need an authorised measurement of actual installed and running fixed versions, with population, denominator and clocks. Benefit caused by discovery would additionally need a defined outcome such as avoided compromise and a credible comparison with what would have happened without this discovery/disclosure, separating later publication, fix availability, deployment and other changes. Neither a severity score, prediction, patch list nor provider exploit demonstration supplies that comparison. The present protocols are not designed to establish it.
