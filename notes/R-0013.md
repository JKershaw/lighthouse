## Findings

This is the second review of the round, alongside notes/R-0012.md, which reviews the four rewritten pieces. Its scope is the site (commit 38fb3ca and the templates after it), the reader journeys on the built site, and every reader- or agent-facing document outside those four pieces, judged against each package's Done-when list. The reviewer read the whole site diff and the test file, ran `npm ci`, `npm test` and `npm run build`, built again with `PATH_PREFIX=/lighthouse/`, built the old commit f5a0021 in a worktree and diffed its legacy pages against the new build, served site/_site and drove it with Chromium through Playwright at 390 and 1280 pixels in light and dark schemes (screenshots in the scratchpad's shots-review/), and read the content files against harbour/README.md, charter.md and the LH010 record's figures as R-0011 confirmed them.

The verdict is that the round can be released, with the four site fixes below made and one About sentence corrected. The site builds cleanly, every internal link on the checked pages resolves (including under a path prefix), legacy study, note and document pages render as before apart from the intended navigation changes, and the content says the same thing at the same certainty across the front page, the investigation page, programme.md, AGENTS.md and the current account. What was wrong was small: listings could not show that a piece had received a "Later evidence" notice, a piece with neither header nor byline would have been dated by its last edit, a mistyped header date would have crashed the build, and two listing ledes still described the short form as a cut of the piece after AGENTS.md made it a standalone brief.

### B. Reader journeys, on the built site at 390 and 1280 pixels, light and dark

- **First visit, /.** One entry point: the question as a heading, a four-sentence lede, "Read the account, or its short form", and the investigation. "Current focus" says what is resting and why, and what comes next, in the same words as programme.md's checkpoint. Nothing else competes; the observation catalogue is one link away on observations.md.
- **Returning visit, /articles/.** Pieces are ordered by publication date with editorial summaries; a draft is tagged; a piece with a `revised` item or a dated notice now says "Revised" or "Later evidence" with the date, and file edits are not shown at all for pieces, so an edit cannot be mistaken for a publication.
- **Arriving on an older piece.** On /articles/what-moves-a-pin/ the byline is followed by "Part of Where does a software update go? · Current account: ... · Short form", then the Later evidence notice linking pins-move-over-weeks and the latest reading, and before the colophon a pager with Previous and Next in the investigation's order. On /articles/pins-move-over-weeks/ the pager leads on to fixes-that-said-so, whose notice leads to the latest reading, whose opening links back to fixes-that-said-so and whose pager leads back; the route works in both directions. The short forms carry the same context among short forms, with a "Full piece" link.
- **/investigations/software-updates/.** Attention ("Active") is set beside the sentence that it says where Lighthouse is looking, not whether anything is proven, and "Where the evidence stands" gives the firmness of each finding separately; the relationships (extended, branched, revised, narrowed) are in the narrative and the six changes of direction are listed.
- **/about/.** Written to a visitor, with the charter, Harbour, who does the work, how it is checked, who holds direction, the operating manual and the programme linked, and the correction channel is the repository's issues, which are the queue the work runs from (issue #12 exists and is open).
- At 390 pixels no page scrolls horizontally; the context line wraps, the pager stacks, and the notice keeps its tint in dark mode. The only console error is the Google Fonts request failing at the sandbox proxy's certificate, which is this environment and not the site.
