**Material claim problems**

1. **The summaries say the loop rests only on agent records, and drop the one thing Amazon's own sensors saw that bears on it.** Passages: LH055.md In short, "it still rests on the attackers' own agent records, whose outcomes Amazon says it can't verify"; investigation page, "its evidence is the agents' own records, captured by its decoy sensors"; programme.md, "every step rests on the agents' own records". A reader comes away believing nothing outside the agents' own logs bears on the loop for Campaign 1, and pictures the decoys as passive recorders. Evidence: S02 line 91 says the agents sent their requests to sensors "configured to present as popular large language model (LLM) inference APIs", and line 98 that they were "treating them as LLM inference backends", sending what frameworks "ordinarily send to an LLM to make operational decisions" (C021, C022; r0093_check_output.txt). Amazon lists five Campaign 1 indicators seen 14 October to 2 November (C048). So the capture itself is Amazon's observation of the agents, Campaign 1's among them on its indicator table, trying to run their work on inference endpoints that weren't theirs, the premise of the loop ("use those models while someone else paid"). The record knows this (L4 paragraph, links.csv S02 L3 and L4, post hoc reading 2) and correctly keeps the links claimed: a decoy is not a stolen key, Amazon doesn't say how the decoys got into the agents' supply (an operator may have configured them) or what they returned, and the decoy vantage selects for this behaviour (post hoc reading 1). But that qualification lives only in the body. The measurements are right; the summary's explanation ("only agent records") is narrower than the evidence and hides the observation round 3 most needs explained. Resolution: in the record's In short, the round 2 checkpoint's opening and the proposed investigation line, add one clause, for example: "Amazon saw those records because the agents sent their working prompts to its decoys, which posed as exposed AI services, as if to run on them; Amazon doesn't say how the decoys came into the agents' supply or what they sent back, so that shows the agents trying to use AI computing that wasn't theirs, not stolen keys paying for anything." Offer the same clause to the publication stage for the reviewed round 1 paragraph and programme.md.
