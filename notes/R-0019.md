## Findings

This is the release gate for LH014, which drew 160 projects at random from two bands of ClickPy's August 2026 ranking (positions 51 to 500 and 501 to 5,000) and applied LH011's two-day measure and LH012's settling test to their April to August 2026 releases, reading no download after 27 September. The reviewer read AGENTS.md, the piece and the short form whole, the record at 0.1, the brief, amendment 1, sources.md and the replay; ran the replay; recounted every tested and quoted figure from the retained reads with its own code (studies/LH014/review/r0019_check.py, output r0019_check.txt), reading analyse.py only for the order of its random draws so that the intervals could be compared exactly; re-read eight project-days live from ClickPy and two upload times from PyPI (review/r0019_live.py, r0019_live.txt); rendered the figure in light and dark; checked every absolute in the titles and summaries, the labelling of what was read after the counts, and every surface the round changed; and ran the site's tests and build. Every figure in the piece, the short form, the record and the surfaces is what the retained files give, and the retained files are what ClickPy and PyPI serve today for the sample read. Nothing had to be fixed for the piece to be released; four wordings in the piece, one in the short form and one on a surface were tightened. The verdict is that the piece and its short form can be released at version 1.0 with the record at 0.1.

### Should fix, fixed

1. **Both summaries.** "For most of the fifty most downloaded Python libraries, most new versions ... were half" counts 26 against 50 where the study's denominator is the 37 that released, which every other surface gives. Now "For 26 of the 37 most downloaded Python libraries that put out new versions between April and August 2026, most of those versions were half of the library's downloads within two days."
2. **Piece, "A smaller share, set early".** "The difference lies more in how large a share a new version took than in how fast" is the piece's reading of the descriptors, stated as fact before its own qualification; now "The difference, we think, lies more in ...".
3. **Piece, the figure's description.** "11 squares below 34": s3transfer's median is 34.0 rounded; now "at 34 or below".
4. **Piece, colophon.** The study directory "follows [the latest reading]" linked the reading of what stays behind under a name this piece now carries on the surfaces; now "the reading of what stays behind".
5. **AGENTS.md, "Current understanding".** "below the top fifty it was not the rule, in 25 of 52 libraries ... and 9 of 35" could be read as the count of libraries where it was not the rule; now "it was not the rule, holding for 25 of 52 libraries ... and 9 of 35".
6. **Record, Method.** One sentence added naming review/ as the place of the release review's recount and live re-reads, as AGENTS.md's Evidence asks; no figure, claim, table or script changed, so the version stays 0.1.
