## Changes

1. **Post hoc paragraph, the "about a third" sentence (piece).** Before: "about a third were about what an instrument counts or who may read it instead (a count that is itself a reading)." After: "about a third were about something else: a quarter about what an instrument counts or who may read it, the rest about data that are dated or out of scope by design (a count that is itself a reading, by one reader)." Why: gap_kinds_output.txt codes 10 definition and 2 access, 12 of 47 coded rows, about a quarter; the third (17 of 47, as the resolution says) includes 5 coded "currency" (dated or out of scope), which is neither what is counted nor who may read it. Mislabelled. Corrected resolution text.
2. **No-limit range (piece).** Before: "17 by our reading (16 to 18 by others)". After: "(14 to 18 by others)". Why: recount_output.txt gives a strict reading of 14 (dropping Hugging Face and Software Heritage, which state a caveat), and R-0050 itself says 14 to 18. 3. **Same range in the record.** Before: "16 to 18 of the 48 by other readings of the borderline rows". After: "14 to 18 ...". Same reason. Corrected resolution text.
4. **Censys's 98 and 62 per cent (piece).** Before: "at 98 per cent of IPv4 services on the most common ports and 62 per cent across all of them." After: "at 98 per cent of IPv4 services on the ten most common ports and 62 per cent across all 65,000 ports, against their own sample from 2024." Why: L01's sentence is "98% of IPv4-based services on the top 10 ports ... 62% of services across all 65K ports", measured against a one-week ZMap sample from 20 August 2024 (literature.json, method). 7. **FIRST (piece).** Before: "The daily exploit-prediction scores published by FIRST, a forum of security teams, are built ...".
