## Findings

### Must-fix

1. **Measure 1, opening span is wrong.** "Openings run from 10 November 13:00 to 12 November 05:00 UTC" contradicts counts.csv and the record's own day table (60 openings on 12 November, 57 of them from 06:00 on). The last held M1 opening is in 2026-11-12-18.
   Proposed: "Openings run from the hour starting 13:00 UTC on 10 November to the hour starting 18:00 UTC on 12 November;"
2. **In short, 655 called "under 1 per cent".** 655 is 1.06 per cent of 61,839 and 1.04 per cent of 63,000. The under-1-per-cent shares in measure 3 are 595 and 606, inside each firm's window. The answer is unchanged ("a small fraction" either way), but the opening is the sentence most read and it must be right.
   Proposed: "Counting GH Archive's public hourly files from 9 to 26 November, we find 595 events opening a pull request from the probe's branch in the hours of one firm's search window and 606 in the other's, under 1 per cent of what each firm's GitHub search counted (655 in all, the first in the hour from 13:00 UTC on 10 November), and over the following two weeks only 6 closing events and 21 branch deletions, in the two hours in five whose files record deletions at all."

### Should-fix

1. **Answer, onset wording.** "The probe branch first appears in the hour 2026-11-10-13" → "The first probe pull request event is in the hour 2026-11-10-13, the hour after the vendors' stated start; the start hour holds none, and branch creations there and in 28 earlier hours are unknown."
2. **"7 more M1 deletions (14 to 21)"** reads like dates. → "7 more M1 deletions (on 18, 22 and 23 November; 21 in all, from 14)".
3. **Measure 6, "single events".** → "the hours added since hold one or two events an hour, scattered over 16 to 26 November".
4. **Limits, "No cause".** "The record rules out only LH046's gap as the explanation for pull request events (measure 7)" → "Interpretation: measure 7 shows that LH046's gap-hour class, which drops branch and push events, doesn't account for the low share, since 652 of the 655 openings are in gap hours. LH046 also found pull request events arriving at roughly half their earlier level in every hour, which this record can't test. A halving alone wouldn't bring a share to under 1 per cent."
