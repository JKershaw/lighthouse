The reviewer's calculation for the installer share (Python 3, standard library), kept here as the record for that figure:

```
import csv, collections
rows = list(csv.DictReader(open('studies/LH005/data/clickpy_mcp_daily_by_installer_window.csv')))
tot = collections.Counter()
for r in rows:
    if r['version_class'] == '1.27.0' and '2026-04-03' <= r['date'] <= '2026-04-22':
        tot[r['installer']] += int(r['downloads'])
s = sum(tot.values())
print(s, [(k or '(blank)', v, round(100 * v / s, 1)) for k, v in tot.most_common(3)])
# 94095825 [('uv', 76466745, 81.3), ('pip', 17280928, 18.4), ('poetry', 267685, 0.3)]
```

### Must fix, fixed

1. **"For the library's next release, 1.28.0 of 16 June"** (synthesis). 1.27.1 (8 May) and 1.27.2 came between 1.27.0 and 1.28.0 (LH002's brief; LH010's events). Now "For a later release of the library".
2. **"the fixes that said so led under every reading we tried"** (synthesis) and **"under every way of counting we tried"** (version 2.0, opening). True over the whole stretch from the release to the advisory under every clock, case set and grouping (18 ratios from 1.25 to 5.96), but in days two to seven with the incidental security words dropped the pooled ratio is below one under every clock (0.69 to 0.79; comparisons.csv), which version 2.0 itself reports as "2.6 against 3.3". Both sentences now say "under every clock and every reading of the notes we tried, counted over the whole stretch from the release to the advisory".
3. **"In the advisories we read, security fixes came out before their advisories"** (synthesis, What held). True of LH008's 68 high or critical advisories as an observation; LH010's wider set had 227 reviewed advisories of which 12 did not have the fix a day earlier, and the 215 were selected on it. Now "In the high or critical advisories we read".
