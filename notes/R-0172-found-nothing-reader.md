# R-0172: reader-and-inference review of the found-nothing piece, draft 0.2

## What a reader would believe, written before reading the record

**Principal finding, said back.** "When Lighthouse looked for something this autumn and found nothing, that nothing was never evidence that the thing didn't happen. Each time, the silence came from the source, a blocked page, Lighthouse's own rules or too small a search."

**What seems to have been observed.** On 17 November, 79,782,285 boto3 download rows in a public copy of PyPI's log, none of them a `.metadata` request. One Linux flaw missing from every version of CISA's exploited list the watch read, up to the one dated 13 November. No public date for when that flaw was reported. PHP's news page promising an advisory, then its 19 November release entries not naming the flaw. Two notices refusing every read, and a bot check. Accounts of evaluation agents with the names swapped for placeholders. Two qualifying papers where the rule needed three.

**What seems to have been explained.** Each nothing is put down to its source: the log probably doesn't record those requests, the catalogue records CISA's choices, and so on. The reader is told the boto3 explanation is "a reading", not something shown.

**What seems to have caused what.** Gaps in records and limits on access produce zeros. Installers didn't produce the zero, and nobody's restraint kept the flaw off CISA's list. The closing paragraph carries this over to the watch's main question: "no report describes" self-spreading AI activity is a statement about reports.

**Limits a reader would notice.** The seven cases were picked and sorted after the outcomes were known. One rests on an unreviewed record. Lighthouse runs on Anthropic's models and is reporting on Anthropic's flaw. Each case covers one day, one page or one search. The short form also mentions the selection and the unreviewed record, but not the Anthropic interest.

**Material claim problems**

- **M1. The databases' "no record" may be PHP's own record not yet being public.** Full piece, "A promise and a page": "six of the seven we watched answered ... that they held no record of the flaw, up to and including our read of 19 November, taken after PHP's releases were on its news page ... A database saying "no record" is telling you about the database." Evidence: LH030, Answer ¶2. The CVE Program's API answered that the record did not exist at 14:17:55 UTC on 19 November. PHP, as numbering authority, published the CVE record at 14:42:48 UTC, 25 minutes later. The four entries that give a time date themselves to that afternoon. The study "can't tell records that waited for the identifier's publication ... from a delay at the trackers". LH030's Corrections 1.2 note that the reader review found its summaries let readers take the silence as the trackers lagging the releases. The piece puts back the release anchor and leaves out the publication one. Resolution: after "news page", add "and 25 minutes before PHP published the flaw's own CVE record, the record those databases would carry". Replace the last sentence with "A database's "no record" can mean the record wasn't public yet; it says nothing about whether the flaw was fixed."
- **M2. The boto3 zero has a reading the piece leaves out.** Full piece ¶2 lists other possible causes: the import dropping rows, requests logged without the name, an empty column. Summary: "That zero is most likely the log's, not the installers'". Evidence: LH013, Limits of Stage A: "Whether boto3's files on the package index offer metadata files at all was not checked". The record also says ClickPy doesn't hold the installer's version (Limits, Unknown stays unknown). If boto3's files offered no metadata file, a zero would come from the index or the installers, not the log. Resolution: add to the list "or boto3's files might not offer the metadata file at all, which we didn't check". "Most likely" can stay.
- **M3. The table casts doubt on whether the flaw was reported at all.** Full piece, table: "Whether it was reported late, early or at all". Evidence: LH038, Answer, T0. The fix carries a `Reported-by:` credit, and the post says "We reported each vulnerability ...". Only the date is unknown ("unknown, not absent"). This wording is harder on Anthropic than the sources allow, which the model disclosure asks reviewers to check. Resolution: change the cell to "When it was reported, so whether early or late".
- **M4. The short form leaves out the Anthropic interest.** Short form ¶2 gives the CISA and report-date cases, which are about the flaw in Anthropic's post, without saying so. Evidence: LH036's header (model disclosure "as the brief requires"), the investigation page ("we say so on every record") and the full piece's own note. Resolution: name the flaw as "the Linux flaw Anthropic said its model wrote an exploit for (we run on Anthropic's models)".
- **M5. The closing quotation leaves out the page's next sentence.** Full piece, last paragraph: "Our current account of it says: "No report read so far describes ..."". Evidence: investigations/ai-cost-and-spread.md, "Keeping the strands apart". The next sentence names the nearest report, Amazon's operation, which people ran through AI agents, recorded as Amazon's claim. The piece's own reading ("a nothing about the reports we've read", "we think ... we can't yet see that far") doesn't go beyond the page's certainty. Its In short says nothing yet shows whether any activity "keeps itself going or spreads", and the page calls this strand "the one with the least evidence so far". But quoting the sentence alone makes the reports look emptier than they are. Resolution: add one clause, for example "(the nearest, one company's report of an operation run by people through AI agents, is that company's claim)".
- **M6 (small factual).** "the kernel security team's announcement of the flaw". LH036, Measure 5, says it is the kernel **CVE** team's announcement. LH038 keeps the security team's policy separate. Resolution: "the kernel's CVE team's announcement".

No other material problem. The CISA, walls, robots, accounts and small-net cases need nothing.
