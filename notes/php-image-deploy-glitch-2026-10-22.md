These are timed reports and a maintainer acknowledgement, not a complete registry availability log. The reported failure was observable after official-images PR #22541 merged at 15:16:45 UTC; LH018's selected fixed-version tags were successfully inspected at 16:49–16:50 UTC. That brackets the observed reports for particular tags, but it cannot establish every affected architecture or exactly when each tag became pullable. LH019's 22 October unchanged amd64 platform digests coexist with later 21 October changes to tag-level push metadata; they do not measure the glitch's duration.

This narrows the practical reading of LH020: a successful PR test and merge did not guarantee immediate availability of every published tag. The public reports warrant a distinct publishing-availability boundary before a consumer rebuild can occur. No uptake percentage follows from the issue's commenters.
