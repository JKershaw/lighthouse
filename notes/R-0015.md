### Must fix, fixed

1. **97, not 98** (piece, "The first day set the month"). "For the eleven, between 63 and 98 per cent" rounded 97.5 a second time; the exact figure is 97.48 (aiobotocore's day-30 median 2.52). Now 97. The record's 97.5 is right at one decimal.
2. **"has not spread slowly"** (piece, same section) and **"not a slow spread"** (short form, body and Corrections line). Both said the low share was not a slow spread, where the record and every summary say "not by itself": the log cannot tell, as the next paragraph says. Now "need not have spread slowly" and "not by itself a slow spread".
3. **"For most of the eleven, the jump came as quickly and was smaller"** (piece). The record's reading is that for nine of the eleven the day-1 median was 70 to 107 per cent of the day-30 median, and the same ratio for the 26 runs from 80 to 110, so "as quickly" held plainly for six. Now: "For nine of the eleven, the jump likewise came on the first day, which brought at least seven tenths of the share the new version would hold for the month; it was simply smaller. For boto3 and litellm the first day brought less of it, 61 and 45 per cent."
4. **The summaries** (piece and short form). Each stated the first-day observation as a plain finding, while the body, README, the notices and the investigation page say it was noticed after the counts and is not yet tested; a summary is read without the body. Each now says it is a pattern seen in almost all of the 37 libraries and noticed only after the counts were in.
5. **3.4, not 3.5** (record, Findings, fifth reading; programme.md's checkpoint). The smallest of the four rises was computed from the rounded shares (57.3 minus 53.8); the exact rise is 3.43 points. Now 3.4 in both. scripts/post_hoc.py prints the rises, so the figure is in the kept output; data/post_hoc_reading.txt was regenerated and the replay rerun and passed.

7. "and the installer then takes an older one without saying so" (piece, "What keeps older versions downloaded"): the clause asserted how installers report a skipped version, which no source in the record was read for; now "takes an older one".
8. The piece's Methods line now says "the other four readings made after the counts", so the piece carries the number of such readings (five with the chart's), as the Evidence rule asks.
9. programme.md's LH011 answer now names the three explanations record 0.3's Limits names, and what was not read (requirements, Requires-Python, the download's Python version), where it had named one.
