## Findings

### Must-fix 1: kernel item contradicts the record on "any fix"

site/this-week.md, the 21 November item beginning "Whether any fix for the kernel flaw". LH038's Answer dates the fix in stable kernels released on 11 and 15 October and the CVE's publication on 15 October, all before the post of 27 October. What stays unknown is whether a SUSE fix was public before the post (LH038, Next: "whether a SUSE fix was public before the post"; AGENTS.md: "whether any SUSE fix was public before the post stays unknown"). The rewrite turns the old question into a flat statement, which makes the error plainer. Replace the sentence before the link with:

> Whether SUSE's fixes for the kernel flaw Anthropic's model found were public before Anthropic's post stays unknown: SUSE's own announcement list dates every SUSE advisory we follow after the post, but their pages carry earlier release dates whose meaning isn't stated.

(Optionally add "The fix itself was in stable kernels from 11 October." if the editor wants the contrast; both dates are in LH038's In short.)

### Must-fix 2: Geneva item states an absence as a fact

site/this-week.md, the 21 November item beginning "No adopted text on autonomous weapons yet:". The checkpoint says what the page didn't list and adds "Absence from the page a day after the close isn't evidence that nothing was adopted" (claim C079); whether anything was adopted "is not established by any source read". The lead clause, in our voice and without "we found", reads as a statement that nothing was adopted. The material is also unreviewed. Replace the opening clause:

> We found no adopted text on autonomous weapons yet: a day after the Geneva review conference of the UN's conventional weapons treaty was due to close, its official page listed no adopted final document and no decision on further talks, and the latest draft we could read still left those passages blank.

(The link and "on 27 November" stay as they are.)

### Must-fix 3: today line picks a referent for "This change"

site/this-week.md, `today:` line, first sentence: "found it says only that the change behind it wasn't caused by AI tooling". comparison.md (version 0.3, after reader review R-0116's material problem 1) says the referent of "This change" is unresolved by the text, names three readings, and calls its own ranking ours. "The change behind it" asserts the first reading. Quoting avoids this and also scopes "only" to AI, which the current wording doesn't (out of context "says only" could read as the report says nothing else). Replace the first sentence with:

> We read Microsoft's report on its November outage, from a consultancy's copy, and found that on AI it says only "This change was not caused by AI tooling".

### Must-fix 4: today line's "its latest" is ambiguous and, read one way, wrong

site/this-week.md, `today:` line, second sentence: "A security firm's tracker of attacks on October's PHP flaws still shows its latest on 20 November." In the 22 November read, the latest confirmed successful exploitation by shell-start date is 20 November (unchanged from 21 November), but the tracker's attacker-address entries run to 22 November (one new address in the last 12 hours) and its page was updated at 13:51 UTC that day. "Its latest" with no noun reads as its latest activity or update, which isn't 20 November. Name the count, in GreyNoise's terms and on its own sensors:

> A security firm's tracker of attacks on October's PHP flaws, on its own decoy servers, still shows no successful attack after 20 November.

(If "successful attack" is felt too loose for GreyNoise's "confirmed successful exploitation", use "still lists its latest successful break-in on 20 November".)

investigations/software-updates.md, new bullet, last sentence: "Set beside PHP's fix in the same survey, a branch-by-branch reading chosen after the figures were seen, it only describes; it can't say whether automatic updates help."
