# LH038 timeline read: 17 November 2026

**In short.** The one timeline read for the Linux flaw CVE-2026-98432 ran on 17 November at 14:19 UTC. It found no public date for when the flaw was reported to anyone. It also found no sign either way that the closed pre-disclosure list for Linux distributions (linux-distros) was used, and no policy text dated differently from the versions read on 4 November. What it did date is the fix: written on 1 October, in stable kernels on 11 and 15 October, then SUSE fixes from 24 October and Red Hat fixes from 1 November. Anthropic's post came on 27 October, 12 to 26 days after those fix dates, depending on which one is the start. This checkpoint is unreviewed. The record is due by 30 November.

Writer: a Claude Code subagent of the scheduled run of 17 November 2026 (Claude Opus 5.5). **Model disclosure:** the exploit and the post are Anthropic's, and this checkpoint was written by an Anthropic model. A review should look for wording that is easier or harder on Anthropic than the sources allow. Unreviewed. This lays dates beside policies. It does not judge whether anyone kept to a policy.

## follow.json, and where the rule could not be applied exactly

[follow.json](../follow.json) was written before the read from LH036's retained read of 16 November ([read log](../../LH036/data/read-2026-11-16T1419Z/read_log.json)). Only the advisory lists from that read were used, as the brief allows. **The rule could not be applied exactly**, for two reasons. These choices were made by this subagent after the trackers' contents, dates included, were visible. They are disclosed here so the driver can record an amendment.

1. **The ceiling binds.** The rule names 244 URLs, and the 80-GET ceiling (the collector asserts it) leaves 52 after the panel's 28:
   - Debian: 0 (its three advisories are already in the panel).
   - Ubuntu: 21 (20 USNs and the "fixes available in proposed pocket" blog post its tracker lists).
   - Red Hat: 27 RHSA errata, taken from `affected_release` and fetched as `https://access.redhat.com/errata/<id>`.
   - SUSE: 189 (186 SUSE-SU announcements and 3 SUSE Liberty list posts).
   - CVE record and OSV references not already in the panel: 7 (four oss-security messages, the cvelistV5 file on GitHub, NVD's detail page and the stable git repository). The eight `git.kernel.org/stable/c/` links were counted as already in the panel, because they are the panel's eight commits by another URL.

   The 52 slots were filled **round robin across these five groups, one each in turn, in each tracker's own listing order**, until the 52 were used. That gave Ubuntu 15, Red Hat 15, SUSE 15 and the references 7. The rule was mechanical and did not choose by date. But the listing orders are the trackers' own, and they are not date order. The 192 URLs not followed are listed below, for the revision the brief schedules after LH036's stop ("follows advisory URLs not followed before").
2. **The oss-security clause takes its input from the read itself.** The month indexes are panel URLs, read in the same run, so their message links could not be known before it. Matching the retained indexes afterwards (code below) gives exactly four messages whose subject contains `CVE-2026-98432`, `MPTCP` or `multipath` (case-insensitive). They are 2026/10/30/1, 10/30/6, 10/31/5 and 11/03/1, the same four that the CVE record's references put in follow.json. So this clause was in effect met, by coincidence of the references rather than by the rule's order of steps. No subject matched `MPTCP` or `multipath` without the CVE identifier.
