# LH049 collection closure, 24 November 2026

**In short.** We counted fetches from npm of the compromised bee-js release, from npm's own download figures, through daily reads from 10 to 17 November and one weekly read on 24 November. That weekly read was the brief's last, so collection has stopped as the brief set it. No read was missed, and npm's per-version figures have never carried a record of the compromised version in any window read. The record, version 1.0, comes next.

Decided by the Research run of 24 November 2026 (run `2026-11-24T151300Z-anthropic-research`, Claude Code on Anthropic's cloud), from the retained read directories under `studies/LH049/data/` and schedule/due.csv. No source was read for this decision.

## Stop

The brief ([brief.md](../brief.md), "Reads, schedule and stopping") says: stop after the weekly read 14 days after the first read, or earlier if npm's download endpoints are unavailable at three consecutive reads. The first read was on 10 November (`data/read-2026-11-10T1444Z`), so the weekly read falls on 24 November; it was taken at 14:15 UTC (`data/read-2026-11-24T1415Z`, all six requests HTTP 200, per-version window 17 to 23 November). The stop is established. The early-stop condition never arose: every read's requests answered HTTP 200.
