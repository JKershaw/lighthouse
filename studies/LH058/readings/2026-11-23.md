The brief takes O1 from the Microsoft outage report watch and lets later reads search for Microsoft's report only if the watch "closes without a qualifying Microsoft-authored report". Overlap: O1 here is the watch's read, not a new one; nothing is counted twice.

From the comparison, as Microsoft's account in the copy (report dated 20 November 2026):

- **Cause**, in the report's words as the comparison quotes them: a latent Windows defect triggered by "the combination of three operational conditions" (p. 2), and "The change that introduced the defect started deployment in July of this year." (p. 6). 22 words of quotation.
- **(a) Security fix:** the comparison describes the change as a Windows connection-handling update on gateway servers and records nothing in the report calling it a security fix or patch for a vulnerability. Recorded as not mentioned, as far as the comparison shows; the report was not read twice to check.
- **(b) Found by an AI model:** not mentioned (the comparison's keyword search of the extracted text found no disclosure of AI use).
- **(c) Statement about AI:** "This change was not caused by AI tooling." (p. 2), with no basis stated; the comparison classes Microsoft's earlier "not related to cyber or AI / agents" line as qualified by it, not confirmed and not contradicted.
- **Kind:** unsettled. The brief's "final" needs the operator to call it a final, full or root-cause report. The comparison quotes the title "Post Incident Report For Microsoft 365" and the report's phrase identifying "the Windows change as the root cause" (p. 8), but doesn't quote the report calling itself final, and the preliminary report came before it. This read leaves the kind for the record (flagged below) rather than deciding it from a summary.
