## Answer

Across many more libraries, the announced fix's lead before the advisory is not found when each library counts once, while pooled over pairs a smaller lead remains that leaving out no single library removes. The top 500 projects gave 43 libraries with a qualifying event, not the 40 or more the design needed: 22 announced and 21 silent, of which 18 announced and 12 silent had pinned dependents to follow (observation; brief.md amendment 1, data/summary_by_class.csv). Their 209 repository and event pairs gave 142 moves by 27 September.

- **Pooled over pairs** (derived; data/hazard_by_class.csv). From the release to the advisory, 2.30 moves per 100 repository-days for announced fixes (35 moves over 1,522.4 repository-days) against 1.57 for silent ones (20 over 1,273.4), about 1.5 times; LH009 had 2.36 against 1.36. In days two to seven after the release, stopping at the advisory, 3.34 (11 moves) against 2.29 (5). Leaving out any one library, the announced rate from the release to the advisory stays between 2.04 and 3.73 and the silent between 1.41 and 1.95.
- **With the library as the unit** (derived; data/library_unit.csv). The median of the libraries' own rates from the release to the advisory is 3.21 for the 18 announced libraries and 2.74 for the 12 silent ones; shuffling the class labels over the 30 libraries 10,000 times gives a difference at least that large, either way, in 89 per cent of shuffles (p = 0.89). In days two to seven the median announced library had no move (median 0.00 over 16 libraries, nine of them with none) against 1.46 for 8 silent libraries (p = 0.68).
- **Share before the advisory** (derived). 35 of 96 moves to announced fixes (36 per cent) and 20 of 46 to silent ones (43 per cent) came before the advisory.
- **Sensitivities** (derived). Security words only (strict class), the library medians are 3.71 against 0.76 (15 libraries each; p = 0.28). With idna's and aiohttp's security words, which concern an earlier release, not counted, the medians are 3.17 against 4.18 (p = 0.78) and the pooled rates 2.17 against 1.74; the pooled lead in days two to seven rests on aiohttp's three moves and turns to 2.61 against 3.30 without them. Within the four libraries where both an announced and a silent fix have followed dependents, the announced fix drew moves faster in three (cryptography, aiohttp, gitpython) and slower in one (pip), on 0 to 4 moves a side. Three events with pairs had an earlier GitHub-reviewed advisory naming the same fixed release that the rule does not see, because it was published before the window or before the fix (nltk, tornado, pip; Findings and Limits). Taking the earliest public advisory naming the fix as the advisory time leaves nltk and pip with no time before it and gives medians of 3.26 for 17 announced libraries against 1.30 for 11 silent ones (p = 0.47), pooled 3.42 (27 moves over 788.5 repository-days) against 1.42 (18 over 1,270.4), 3.04 to 4.02 and 1.25 to 1.74 leaving one library out; in days two to seven, medians 0.00 against 1.46 (14 and 8 libraries, p = 0.67) and pooled 3.28 (9) against 2.29 (5); 27 of 96 announced moves and 18 of 46 silent ones came before that advisory (derived by the reviewer from data/lags.csv and data/osv_advisories.csv with analyse.py's rules; not in the tables).

What this says (interpretation). Pooled over pairs, the announced class still leads before the advisory, by less than in LH009, but the lead is not a property of the typical library: half the announced libraries' dependents moved before the advisory at no more than about three per 100 repository-days, as did half the silent libraries', and a permutation of labels over libraries cannot tell the classes apart. The fastest libraries in each class are small frames with few repository-days: tornado, gitpython, poetry and pypdf among the announced, and pydantic-ai-slim, pip, awscli and fastmcp among the silent, several on one to five moves. The test has little power: 30 libraries, most with a handful of pairs, and many with no move in a window at all. The design's twenty libraries a class with pinned dependents is out of reach in the 500 most downloaded projects in this window. The readings of the notes and of the advisory time move the medians both ways and none is significant; what holds under every reading is that the pooled announced rate is the higher and the typical libraries cannot be told apart. A move is a request in a repository, not an installation or a run, and no project here is said to have been exposed.

## Method

The brief (brief.md) was written before any list of projects, advisory, note, dependents list or repository was read for this study, with the library and event rule (LH008's widened to every severity and the top 500), LH009's classification rule as amended, the order, frame, kept and move rules, the measures, the reuse of LH008's and LH009's reads, and the ceilings. Then, in order: select_libraries.py (ClickPy, OSV, PyPI release lists; the events); classify.py (PyPI's description and the libraries' changelogs and tags for 94 fixes); date_head_entries.py (the one entry first written after its tag); the flaw-named judgements, made by this agent against each fix's advisory summaries and written to data/flaw_judgements.csv; classes.py (data/fix_classes.csv, last written 10:02:16 UTC); amendment 1, whose tables (data/fix_classes.csv and data/frame_defs.csv) were written before the first dependents list was requested at 10:03:13 and whose text was written after the dependents were read (brief.md was last written at 10:20:43; the file times are the working tree's, as the reviewer found them); osv_ranges.py (the frames and their advisories' ranges); collect.py frame, screen and moves (LH009's, with 20 packages a frame, a pair's own fix from the range that holds it, and the Co-authored-by names read in place); reuse.py (the twelve reused frames' first 20 packages, re-tested against LH010's advisory ranges, which kept every earlier kept package); analyse.py; summarise_reads.py. Every table in data/ is written by them; classes.py, reuse.py and analyse.py need no network. The clones are not kept.

Choices made where the brief left room: the library-unit windows are the two the brief names; each library's rate is its own pooled rate, not a mean of its pairs; the permutation counts a shuffle when its absolute difference of medians is at least the observed one (to 1e-12); a third grouping (incidental security words counted silent) is named in amendment 1, and analyse.py, which carries it, was last written at 10:05:01 UTC, after the dependents were listed and cloned and before data/moves.csv was written at 10:18:25, so on the file times it was fixed before any move was known; the reused frames keep their own end of observation (their clone times), so the pairs end between 07:24 and 10:05 UTC on 27 September.

## Limits

- **Too few libraries.** The design asked for 20 libraries a class with pinned dependents; the top 500 projects and five months of advisories gave 18 and 12. Many fixes followed their release before by days, and Open Source Insights lists few or no dependents for such a release, so the frame rule loses exactly the libraries that release often.
- **Low power.** Thirty libraries, most with 2 to 14 pairs and 0 to 5 moves in a window. A permutation p of 0.89 says the typical libraries are alike here; it does not show that a modest real difference is absent. No test was run within a window of the pooled rates, as the brief says.
- **The judgement matters in places.** Four events are announced on the flaw named alone (mako, uv, authlib, mistune), two of them borderline; dropping the ground moves them to silent, where they contribute no move before the advisory, and the library medians part further (3.71 against 0.76, p = 0.28). The word rule itself counts security words about something else (idna, aiohttp 3.13.4); not counting them reverses the medians (3.17 against 4.18) and removes the pooled lead in days two to seven. Both are reported beside the class; neither is significant.
- **Silent in the sources read.** Eight silent events have no notes in the changelog at the tag, the description or the annotation; GitHub's release pages were not requested, since LH009 found every one refused, and such projects often write their notes only there. Security mailing lists, blogs and social media were not read.
- **Class is still partly library.** The within-library comparison has four usable libraries with 0 to 4 moves a side; it points the same way as the pooled rates in three and cannot separate class from library.
- **Short windows.** Where the advisory came within two days of the fix (cryptography, pip, anthropic, pypdf, pydantic-ai-slim and fastmcp; mako's an hour after), there is no days-two-to-seven window and the release-to-advisory rate stands on a few repository-days.
- **Advisories the window does not see.** The rule takes the first advisory published in the window whose fix came a day or more before it; an earlier GitHub-reviewed advisory naming the same fix, published before 27 March or before the fix, is not the event's advisory. That happened for three libraries with pairs (nltk, tornado, pip; Findings), so nltk's 691 repository-days and four of tornado's five moves "before the advisory" came after a public advisory for the same release, and nltk's weight in the pooled announced class is the rule's artefact. The sensitivity in the Answer takes the earliest public advisory instead: the pooled lead widens and the medians part, without significance. A later study should fix the advisory time as the earliest public advisory naming the fix, whatever its date.
- **Frames cut to 20.** The reused frames lose the pairs beyond their first 20 packages in hash order that LH008 and LH009 read, so the pooled figures for those libraries differ from LH009's.
- **Three readings on one day.** LH008's frames were cloned at 07:24, LH009's at 08:49 and LH010's at 10:03 UTC on 27 September; each pair ends at its own clone time.
- **Authorship from git alone**, with LH008's limits; pull-request bodies and labels were not read. **A move is a request.** Nothing here says what was installed or run, or whether any project was exposed.

## Next

The question needs more libraries than the most downloaded 500 hold in five months: either a longer window of advisories, which LH008's rule allows, or a frame that does not depend on the release before the fix having dependents listed (for example the dependents of the library as a whole, screened for a pin on an affected version). GitHub's release pages, read from an address that is served them, would settle whether the eight silent events without notes were silent. And whether the people who moved in the week after an announced release had read its notes remains a question for their pull requests.
