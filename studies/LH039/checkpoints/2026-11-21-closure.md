# LH039 collection closure, 21 November 2026

**In short.** We watched how fast websites took up WordPress's fix of 28 October, reading two views that see releases every day from 9 to 21 November. Collection has now stopped as the brief set it: every daily read and both developer-download checkpoints were taken, and none is missing. The one gap is WordPress.org's own branch statistics, which its robots.txt has refused to us since 9 November; they are missing, not zero. The analysis of these reads comes next, then the record.

## Actual attempts and gaps

Each daily read from 9 to 21 November logged 20 requests: 14 answered HTTP 200 (WordPress.org's download counter and per-branch download endpoints, W3Techs's WordPress, 7.x, 7.1, 7.0 and 6.9 pages and their histories, and Packagist's two package summaries) and 6 were not fetched, the same six every day: api.wordpress.org's four statistics endpoints, its version-check and its stable-check, refused by that host's robots.txt (group `*`, `Disallow: /`) under the amendment of 9 November. Both Packagist checkpoints logged 52 requests, all HTTP 200, within the 52-GET bound.

So the measures that rest on api.wordpress.org are missing from 9 November onward, not negative and not "no change"; W3Techs and Packagist reads are complete for every scheduled day. No read was backfilled, extended or repeated for this decision.
