# LH035 survey table

Built by `python3 studies/LH035/scripts/build.py` from studies/LH035/data/ (instruments.json, candidates.json, literature.json, fetchlog.csv); edit the data, not this file. Rows I01 to I40 were filled from the instrument's own pages fetched on 2 November 2026 with WebFetch, which returns a model-written extract of each page rather than the page itself; in them, quotation marks enclose the extract's quotation of the page. Rows I41 to I48 were filled on 5 November 2026 from raw pages fetched with curl and saved in data/reads-2026-11-05/, so their quotations were checked against the page itself, except where a row says it rests on a 2 November extract. Each row says how and when it was read. "not stated" means the pages read did not give it. The last column but one, the characteristic blind spot, is Lighthouse's reading (interpretation), not the instrument's statement, and may be blank. Layer numbers are the brief's: 1 Physical and interconnection; 2 Routing; 3 Addresses and reachability; 4 Names; 5 Certificates; 6 Running services and their software; 7 Web content and technology; 8 Software supply; 9 Vulnerability and exploitation records; 10 Traffic.

Origin: a = an instrument Lighthouse's studies used; b = an LH003 row touching a layer here (re-read, not copied); c = the fixed search plan (search ids as in searchlog.md). Literature ids (L01 to L11) are in the table near the end; a paper's evaluation is cited beside the row it concerns.
