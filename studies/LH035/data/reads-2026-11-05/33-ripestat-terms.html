RIPEstat Service Terms and Conditions

Introduction

This document describes the Terms and Conditions under which the RIPE NCC makes the RIPEstat Service available to the RIPE community.

Article 1 – Definitions

In these Terms and Conditions, the following shall be understood to have the meaning assigned to them below:

RIPE NCC – Réseaux IP Européens Network Coordination Centre, a membership association under Dutch law, with registered office in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

RIPEstat Service – A RIPE NCC service offering access to the RIPEstat Data via the web-based interface on the Website, the RIPEstat Widgets or the RIPEstat Data API. 

RIPEstat Data – Current and historical Internet-related data collected by the RIPE NCC, the other RIRs or third parties and provided in a unified form via the RIPEstat Service.
A list of the various RIPEstat Data sources is available on our Website. This list may be updated from time to time to reflect any relevant changes.

The Website – All webpages hosted under the ripe.net domain and any sub-domains thereof.

RIPEstat Widgets – Interactive graphical and visual representations of the RIPEstat Data that can be embedded on other websites by using the provided embedding code.

RIPEstat Data API – The RIPEstat Data Application Programmable Interface (API) that allows the Users to directly interact with the RIPEstat Service.

User(s) – Any person(s) accessing, querying, browsing, viewing, downloading or interacting either directly or through the intermediation of automated means, in any way with (part of) the RIPEstat Service.

Use – Access, query, browse, view, download or interact either directly or through the intermediation of automated means in any way with (part of) the RIPEstat Service.

RIPE community – RIPE (Réseaux IP Européens) is a collective forum open to all parties interested in wide area IP networks in Europe and beyond. The objective of RIPE is to ensure the administrative and technical coordination necessary to enable the operation of a pan-European IP network.

RIR – A Regional Internet Registry that has the authority to register Internet number resources within their respective service region.

Article 2 – General

2.1 The Terms and Conditions come into effect by means of an offer by the RIPE NCC and an acceptance by the User.

2.2 By using the RIPEstat Service, the User confirms that they have read, understood and agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If the User does not agree with these Terms and Conditions, they shall not make use of the RIPEstat Service.

2.3 These Terms and Conditions may be subject to change without prior notice and will be valid upon publication unless specifically indicated otherwise by the RIPE NCC. By continuing to use the RIPEstat Service after any changes are made to the Terms and Conditions, Users confirm that they have read, understood and agree to be bound to these changes.

2.4 These Terms and Conditions replace any and all existing agreements between the RIPE NCC and the User with regards to the RIPEstat Service. 

2.5 The Terms and Conditions prevail over explanatory documents and any text regarding the RIPEstat Service, which exist for guidance and informational purposes only and do not affect the interpretation of these Terms and Conditions.

Article 3 – Use of the RIPEstat Service

3.1 The RIPE NCC makes the RIPEstat Service available for purposes such as network analysis, network monitoring and debugging and research (hereafter referred to as the purpose of the RIPEstat Service).

3.2 Users agree to make use of the RIPEstat Service in line with the purpose of the RIPEstat Service as mentioned in Article 3.1 above. If Users perform research using the RIPEstat Service, they must notify the RIPE NCC about the results of this research by sending an email to stat@ripe.net . In such a case the User must also attribute the RIPE NCC and the source of the RIPEstat Data (if different than the RIPE NCC) when they make publicly available their research results generated by any use of the RIPEstat Service.

3.3 A User may not re-package, compile, re-distribute the RIPEstat Service and any (or all) of the RIPEstat Data. Use of the RIPEstat Service for commercial purposes, such as by providing paid services, products or any other derivatives that are based on the RIPEstat Service, or by incorporating the RIPEstat Data with other sources of data and packaging it as commercial product is not allowed unless permission to do so is granted in writing by the RIPE NCC. For such a request, a User may contact the RIPE NCC by sending an email to stat@ripe.net.

Article 4 – Control of Use

4.1 The RIPE NCC has the right to decommission, add, combine any of the RIPEstat Data or alter the form via which the RIPEstat Data is provided according to its own needs, obligations, interests and interests of the RIPE community.

4.2 The RIPE NCC is entitled to restrict any use of the RIPEstat Service. The RIPE NCC may perform reasonable security checks and audits to determine unauthorised use of the RIPEstat Service.

4.3 The Use of the RIPEstat Data is subject to these Terms and Conditions. Specific RIPEstat Data may be subject to additional terms and conditions. In case there is a conflict between these Terms and Conditions and the additional terms and conditions or disclaimers, the additional terms and conditions or disclaimers will prevail over these Terms and Conditions with regards to that particular RIPEstat Data.
