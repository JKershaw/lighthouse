urlscan.io: A sandbox for the web

urlscan.io is a free service to scan and analyze websites. We browse to a URL like a regular user and record everything that the page navigation creates, so that anyone can easily and confidently analyze unknown and potentially malicious websites.

Our Mission

Bringing the community and defenders together

Every day, researchers, analysts and automated pipelines around the world submit millions of URLs to urlscan.io. We want to connect this community with the defenders who protect their organizations and customers, so that what one person discovers can help everyone else.

Collecting data on threats

We record what websites actually do and make it searchable, so that phishing, malware and fraud infrastructure can be found, tracked and taken down. Our detections are organic: they come from our own scanning and research, not from aggregating other vendors' verdicts.

Context matters

A single observation is often meaningless without the necessary context. Is this domain something that websites usually load third-party JavaScript from? Are any other reputable websites talking to this weird IP address on the Cayman Islands?

A sandbox for URLs

Just like you would use a malware sandbox to analyze suspicious files, you can use urlscan.io to do the same thing, but with URLs. We break down a page navigation into digestible chunks and anticipate the attributes that allow pivoting during an investigation.
