# LH048 reads: 10 November 2026 (discovery and baseline)

**In short.** We matched the flaws in Anthropic's five attack chains to the two vendor pages its post links, by a rule fixed beforehand, and then took the first reading of how each identifiable flaw is rated and whether it shows signs of exploitation. Four flaws can be followed: the named Windows and Linux ones, one Safari flaw that Apple credits to Anthropic, and one Chrome flaw that could be either of two steps. None is in CISA's catalogue of exploited flaws; all four are rated High or Critical by at least one source; their exploit-prediction scores are low. Mondays from 16 November say whether any of that moves. Unreviewed working notes.

Model disclosure: the exploits and the post are Anthropic's, and this checkpoint was written by a Claude Code session on Claude Opus 5.5, an Anthropic model.
