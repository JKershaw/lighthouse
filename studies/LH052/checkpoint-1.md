# From outage accounts to internal measurements

**In short.** The first iteration found readable accounts for two of four selected outages, with three documents in total. They describe technical failures and recovery but do not connect AI-motivated staffing changes to lost expertise and operational outcomes. We will use the protocol's second and final iteration to inspect the two preselected operator studies for the measurements missing here.

Decision: 13 November 2026, after iteration-1.md, cases.csv and the source records were written, before iteration 2 collection. Driver: Codex, OpenAI; exact model and monetary usage unavailable. This is the conditional decision specified by the brief, not a new hypothesis test.

Continuing to add known outages would widen a selected failure panel without supplying staffing exposure or an unaffected comparison. Repairing the measurement means asking what internal AI-exposure, review and incident records the two operator papers actually contain. An outward study of another AI mechanism could be valuable but would not close this gap. We choose the measurement audit and stop after it, within the fixed document, search and effort bounds.

Only Meta RADAR (arXiv 2605.30208) and Mockus et al. (DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.ESEM.2026.93) enter iteration 2. At most one author/publisher record and full paper per study, gated before reading, four documents total. No new case, outreach or recurring watch. Their prior-known outcomes are disclosed in the brief. Four of the eight discovery queries have been used; resolve these known URLs directly where possible and reserve the remaining queries for a genuine resolution need or verification.

The second iteration audits evidence coverage, selection and claims; it does not replicate private operational data. Inaccessible papers remain unavailable, and no report of no association will be described as a demonstration of safety. The first iteration and this checkpoint will be committed locally before collection resumes. Public publication remains blocked as recorded in the commissioning note.
