# Can public records link AI, organisational capacity and infrastructure reliability?

**In short.** The readable outage accounts describe technical failure and recovery, and the two operator papers report measurements of AI code, review and incidents. None of these audited materials establishes a chain from AI-motivated staffing changes through lost expertise to an outage. This bounded pilot stops after two iterations, with a clearer measurement question rather than a finding that the organisational mechanism is absent.

## Answer

The proposed organisational mechanism fits Lighthouse's questions about AI activity, maintenance and recovery, but the evidence types answer different questions. A product using AI, software retry traffic and an organisational decision justified by AI are different exposures. Public outage accounts can disclose one without the others. Internal code and incident telemetry can improve measurement without identifying lost understanding or a staffing effect.

This result describes what these selected reports could and could not establish, not an estimate of outage frequency, a Microsoft attribution or a conclusion about AI's net effect. Harbour's essays and the prior literature review motivated the question; they are not independent measurements in this pilot.

The outage evidence is in [iteration 1](iteration-1.md), [the source-field matrix](cases.csv) and [its source manifest](sources-iteration-1.json), collected on 13 November. The papers are audited in [iteration 2](iteration-2.md), [its measurement matrix](operator-measurements.csv) and [source manifest](sources-iteration-2.json), collected on the same date. Those records retain event and publication/version clocks separately from collection. The GitHub event was 17 August 2026 and Cloudflare's 18 November 2025; the two unread cases remain in the panel as unavailable.

**Review pressure is a measurable question; expertise loss needs another measure.** RADAR reports code growth and reduced timely review, then describes selected low-risk review automation. Its comparisons are observational and selected, and its knowledge-transfer assurance is not accompanied by a reported measure of retained understanding in the audited text. We do not repeat its mathematically unclear percentage reduction in closing time. These qualifications apply to this paper, not to every automated-review system. [I2-S01/I2-S02; matrix I2-M01-M14]

Mockus and colleagues study pre-agentic assistance over about two months in spring 2025, analysing the roughly half of non-automation diffs with provenance coverage. Their main text distinguishes the raw incident comparison from a small adjusted negative association. We preserve that distinction instead of adopting the abstract's broader lower-incident wording. The design does not establish causal safety or the effect of replacing experienced staff with agents. [I2-S03/I2-S04; matrix I2-M15-M28]

Both papers concern Meta, with overlapping authors. They are not independent company replications. Review time is not understanding, company tenure is not all relevant experience, and a severe-incident association does not cover every defect or recovery skill.

## Method

The [brief](brief.md) was retained before new collection. All four outage outcomes and both operator papers were known in outline beforehand. The purposive panel cannot supply a prevalence denominator or an unaffected comparison. After the first record was written, [the checkpoint](checkpoint-1.md) activated the protocol's conditional measurement audit and was committed before that collection. No extra cases, replacements, outreach or recurring watch were added.

There were nine unique attempted substantive endpoints, seven readable documents and four discovery queries. Collection logs record 21 HTTP requests, including robots reads and retention-policy inspections, 2,017,506 response-body bytes and 135.353 seconds summed measured HTTP duration. Search backend activity and work outside measured requests are not covered. The evidence reviewer used seven cached primary documents, with no new HTTP requests or searches. [Offline replay](review/evidence-check-combined.py) and [recorded output](review/evidence-check-combined.json) retain the counting rules. These are collection footprints, not total compute or billing.

Full bodies are temporary verification material. The repository retains short located paraphrases, coding data and original-response hashes, which cannot reproduce private operational facts or a full source. The Google and AWS gate received HTML placeholders rather than authentic robots policies; the collector recognised this after the gated substantive request and stopped, with no alternate route. A second collector temporarily saved a publisher HTML cache before email redaction; evidence review caught it and the cache was corrected, with original and redacted hashes distinguished. The iteration records retain these access, clock and retention limitations rather than treating them as successful compliant observations.

The [fresh primary-source review](../../notes/LH052-evidence-review.md) found no unresolved must-fix after its corrections. The [two-stage reader-and-inference check](../../notes/LH052-reader-review.md) found no material inference problem. The [fresh changed-claim recheck](../../notes/LH052-final-recheck.md) also passed after its two obsolete-status corrections, applied verbatim. These are contributions from the same model family, not independent confirmations.

## Limits

Half the outage cases were inaccessible. All were selected with prior outcome knowledge, and readable operator accounts remain selective reports. The papers do not expose private telemetry for replication, and their selection, incomplete provenance and uncertain cohort overlap limit transfer. A missing staffing measure is neither evidence of preserved capacity nor evidence of lost capacity. Ordinary software automation, demand changes, architecture, work mix and reporting omissions remain alternatives.

The broader 26-source review is retained as [commissioning background](../../notes/organisational-reliability/background-review.md); this pilot does not newly verify all of it. No claim of current harm, compromised infrastructure or Microsoft staffing causation is made. Exact runtime model, token totals, complete active-time metering and price are unavailable. No monetary ceiling can be certified from those gaps; actual billing remains John's control.

## Next

Continuing to add known outage narratives is unlikely to supply the missing team-level exposure. Repairing measurement would require linked staffing and AI provenance, relevant service knowledge and review activity, demand and change risk, and incident/recovery outcomes, with a credible comparison and coverage disclosure. Another outward question, such as agent-generated load, remains distinct and should use its own protocol.

We recommend keeping this thread in the existing shared-internet costs investigation and stopping collection here. A possible next design would first assess whether Harbour's own records can measure production versus verification and rework. That would be an agent-workflow case, not a test of departures among employees; effective understanding would still need a separate instrument. No data access, new experiment or outreach is commissioned by this suggestion. Issue [#51](https://github.com/JKershaw/lighthouse/issues/51) retains the next decision and publication handoff.
