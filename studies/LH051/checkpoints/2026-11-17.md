## Where the brief was ambiguous, and how it was implemented

Nothing in the brief was changed. Choices the text left open:

1. **Distinct counts (measure 1).** The brief asks for distinct repositories and actors "per hour and marker"; the collector formed them per hour, marker, type and action, and they cannot be added across cells. The tabulator reports the cells as written and forms no per-hour total.
2. **Review event actions.** The brief's action classes (opened, closed, reopened, other) are for pull request events; the collector kept PullRequestReviewEvent's own actions (created, updated). They are reported as written. Measures 3, 5 and 7 use PullRequestEvent only, as the brief says.
3. **Onset (measure 2)** is the first held hour holding an M1 event, which in gap hours means a pull request or review event only.
4. **Measure 4** sums both series over the non-gap hours from onset to 23:00 on 12 November; it does not pair them hour by hour.
5. **"Last complete day" (measure 5)** is taken as a UTC day with all 24 hours held (15 November). "Closed" is GH Archive's action, which includes merged pull requests; the files' merged flag is not read. Deletions count all ref_types (all M1 deletions held are branches) and only in non-gap hours. The cumulative ratio runs from 10 November.
6. **Measure 6** is reported by event type, with hours where a count is unknown (gap hours, for creations, deletions and pushes) left out of the median and range rather than counted as zero; "after" runs from 2026-11-10-12 to the last held hour.
7. **Measure 7** reports totals by action and hour counts. The per-hour means and the split at 13 November in the output are descriptive additions, not in the brief, and test nothing.
