## Method

`take.json`, already held in this study's scratch directory, was parsed with Python's `json` module for an exact field count, rather than transcribed by hand. The host was sampled twice with `date -u +%FT%TZ`, `cat /proc/loadavg`, `nproc`, `ps -eo pid,pcpu,pmem,etimes,comm --sort=-pcpu | head -12`, and a count of `/proc/net/tcp` lines in state `01`, since `ss` and `netstat` are both absent here. Raw output is quoted in full in `observations.md`, with the observation record table. Processes named in `ps` were identified by reading `/proc/<pid>/cmdline` directly, never by calling a service.

`grounded_at` came from `git -C /home/user/lighthouse rev-parse --short HEAD`, taken again near the end of writing: HEAD had advanced from `a408407` through unrelated driver commits, including one that captured an earlier draft of this study under the same path.
