Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons — Seventh Review Conference

(2026)

Documents Statements

Overview

The Seventh Review Conference of the High Contracting Parties to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) will take place from 16 to 20 November 2026 at the Palais des Nations in Geneva. It will be presided by H.E. Mr. Julien Thöni, Permanent Representative of Switzerland to the Conference on Disarmament.

Participants at the meeting are invited to read and familiarize themselves with the Code of Conduct to prevent harassment, including sexual harassment, at UN system events.

Information for Participants

On 9 October 2026, the CCW Implementation Support Unit circulated an aide-mémoire providing information on attending the Seventh Review Conference of the CCW.

Credentials

On 29 April 2026, UNODA Geneva circulated Notes Verbales from the Secretary-General convening the Seventh Review Conference of the CCW to Permanent Missions in Geneva.

Speaking time limits

In order to facilitate the orderly conduct of proceedings and ensure that all delegations have the opportunity to take the floor, statements delivered under the General Exchange of Views will be limited to five minutes per delegation. For all other agenda items, statements will be limited to three minutes per delegation.

Incendiary Weapons and White Phosphorus Munitions: Humanitarian Impacts and a Call for ActionSide event organized by Human Rights Watch and Norwegian People's Aid. Concept-note.

This side event will examine the effects of incendiary weapons and white phosphorus munitions as the Review Conference of the Convention on Conventional Weapons (CCW) prepares to address Protocol III. Panelists will detail the humanitarian, legal, medical, operational, and environmental consequences of these weapons, exemplified by use in conflicts ranging from Vietnam to Lebanon. Panelists will present the findings of two new reports by Human Rights Watch and Norwegian People’s Aid. They will also call on states to take concrete action at the CCW Review Conference to advance protections for civilians from these cruel weapons.
