Seventh Review Conference of the High Contracting Parties to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW)

10 November 2026 Press Release

UNITED NATIONS OFFICE IN GENEVA, 10 November 2026 – The Seventh Review Conference of the High Contracting Parties to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) will be held at the Palais des Nations in Geneva from 16 - 20 November 2026 under the presidency of Ambassador Julien Thöni (Switzerland).

The Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) is a key international humanitarian law instrument that seeks to ban or restrict the use of specific types of weapons that are considered to cause unnecessary or unjustifiable suffering to combatants or to affect civilians indiscriminately. Its five Protocols address a range of conventional weapons, including non-detectable fragments, mines and improvised explosive devices, incendiary weapons, blinding laser weapons and explosive remnants of war. It currently has 128 High Contracting Parties. 

Held every five years, the Review Conference provides an important opportunity to assess the implementation, scope and operation of the Convention and its existing Protocols, consider developments in the means and methods of warfare and their humanitarian implications, and set priorities for future work. 

A central issue will be Lethal Autonomous Weapon Systems (LAWS). The Review Conference will consider the outcome of the Group of Governmental Experts (GGE), established under the Convention in 2016 to address these weapon systems, and decide on the future work on this issue under the CCW.

Opening segment 

The Seventh Review Conference will open on Monday 16 November at 10:00 a.m. (Geneva time) in the Assembly Hall at the Palais des Nations in Geneva.
