## Question, population and units

For three purposively selected cases, what can public evidence establish about armament, AI functions, human control, maturity of deployment and civilian consequences? Over the pilot, what material claims or documented states change within a fixed source panel?

The cases are Foundation/Phantom and its proposed Gaza application; one armed non-humanoid comparison; and Changi Airport's patrol robots as a civilian-authority comparison, with armament not established by the sources read. The unit is a claim or event about a specified platform configuration, institution and context. Each function can differ by mode, version and customer. Do not assign a company a single autonomy level.

This is a descriptive case study and a test of public observability, not a survey of the world's weapons or a causal test of whether robots reduce harm. The two comparisons are classification checks, not a control group from which effects can be estimated.

## Events, clocks and coding

Use [events.csv](events.csv) and [claims.csv](claims.csv), following [the data dictionary](README.md). Every coded substantive statement links a source ID, the exact retained read ID and a passage locator. Separate the following:

1. **Function:** navigation; perception/tracking; recommendation; target selection; engagement; abort/intervention. For each record human-controlled, automated, mixed or unknown, and the evidence of AI involvement separately. "Human in the loop" alone is an attributed phrase, not a complete control description.
2. **Maturity event:** proposal, demonstration, trial, award/purchase, delivery, operational use, suspension, withdrawal or cancellation. No stage implies another and there is no inevitable progression.
3. **Authority:** mission setter, target selector, force authoriser, reported intervention mechanism, operational constraints, policy commitments and evidence of practice. An undocumented field stays unknown.
4. **Civilian consequences:** reported harm, coercion, privacy or movement restrictions, complaints, investigations and remedies. Separately code harm evidence, operator/platform attribution and attribution to a particular AI function. Do not join them by proximity in time or place.
5. **Economics:** currency, price/award date, amount or quantity as reported, whether announced/awarded/delivered, scope, inclusions and unknown support/supervision costs. Do not calculate a pooled unit price from incompatible figures.

Clocks: keep the source's publication and revision dates, the event date as the source gives it, and Lighthouse's UTC read time separately. A change is first observed between the previous readable snapshot and the current one. A source's date is not proof of first public visibility. Do not backdate a late snapshot to a scheduled date.

Evidence labels describe provenance: supplier claim, official account, third-party report, or reproducible artifact. Record corroborating or contradictory source families rather than assigning an overall trust score. Primary status is claim-specific: an official announcement establishes that the institution made that announcement, not that a capability works.

- A final statement of what this method can and cannot observe, with collection/review effort. No test of the economics-of-power hypothesis is promised; list the evidence a separate test would require.

Plausible alternative readings include supplier ambitions failing to become deployments, AI confined to navigation, human control differing between configurations, withdrawals or public resistance constraining use, and protective applications benefiting civilians. The record must preserve evidence against expansion as carefully as evidence for it. New facts about function, delivery, withdrawal, harm or redress could change a case's conclusion.

## Resource envelope and uncertainty

Agent-usage ceiling $100 where attributable usage can be measured. Log time and known/unknown model costs in the checkpoint; unknown is not zero and this ceiling is not a representation of subscription billing. Reassess before continuing if measured usage reaches $80 or effort reaches 12 hours; stop by the hard envelope unless John extends it. Do not start a paid service.

Baseline bound: 30 documents. Each weekly read: at most 12 endpoints, plus at most one connection-failure retry per endpoint, no retries around walls; six ordinary reads. Extra reads are limited above and share the same resource envelope. Use bounded sequential requests, not a crawler.

Inconclusive outcomes include inaccessible reporting or procurement records, unknown configuration, contradictory accounts, absent civilian-attribution evidence, or an inadequate baseline. A well-supported account of those gaps can still be useful. The study cannot establish worldwide prevalence, comparative battlefield effectiveness, a causal effect on civilian harm, or the total cost of operating robotic forces.
