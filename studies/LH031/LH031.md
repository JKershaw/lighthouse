**Study:** LH031, version 0.1 (first read), 23 October 2026. **Writer:** a Claude Code driving session (Claude Opus 5.5). **Protocol:** [brief](brief.md), commit `738a251`, before the read. **Observation:** 23 October 2026 at about 18:45 UTC ([read log](data/read-2026-10-23T1845Z/read_log.json), [extract](data/read-2026-10-23T1845Z/result.json)). **Instrument:** [collect.py](scripts/collect.py); CISA's own feed answered, so the mirror was not read.

## Answer, at the first read

CISA's feed, catalogue version 2026.10.23 with a release time of 23 October 17:00:09 UTC and 1,755 entries, lists none of CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619 or CVE-2026-115560. That is CISA's classification as of that release, two days after the fixes and after Amazon's and Cloudflare's public claims of exploitation attempts (LH028); it says nothing about whether exploitation occurred. Later reads will show whether and when any is added, by CISA's `dateAdded` and by our reads.

## Limits

An agency's catalogue applies its own evidence threshold, which is not published per entry; its absence of an entry is not a negative finding. The feed's release time is CISA's clock.
