# When, if ever, does CISA's catalogue of known exploited vulnerabilities list the October 2026 PHP flaws?

**Study:** LH031, version 0.2, 27 October 2026 (version 0.1, first read, 23 October). **Writer:** a Claude Code driving session (Claude Opus 5.5). **Protocol:** [brief](brief.md), commit `738a251`, before the read. **Observation:** twelve reads from 23 October 2026 at about 18:45 UTC ([read log](data/read-2026-10-23T1845Z/read_log.json), [extract](data/read-2026-10-23T1845Z/result.json)) to 27 October 2026 at 06:34 UTC, each with its own read log in data/. **Instrument:** [collect.py](scripts/collect.py); CISA's own feed answered, so the mirror was not read.

## Answer, at the read of 27 October

CISA's feed, read at 06:34 UTC on 27 October ([extract](data/read-2026-10-27T0633Z/result.json), [read log](data/read-2026-10-27T0633Z/read_log.json)), is catalogue version 2026.10.26 of the "CISA Catalog of Known Exploited Vulnerabilities" (the feed's own title), with a release time of 26 October 22:24:24 UTC and 1,758 entries. It lists CVE-2026-109618 ("PHP Memory Corruption Vulnerability") and CVE-2026-109619 ("PHP Stale Internal Request-State Vulnerability"), each with `dateAdded` 2026-10-26, `dueDate` 2026-10-29, `forensicTriage` "Yes" and `knownRansomwareCampaignUse` "Unknown"; it does not list CVE-2026-115560. Every read from 23 October through 26 October at 18:34 UTC found catalogue version 2026.10.23 and none of the three; the daily and intraday checkpoints in [checkpoints/](checkpoints/) give each read. So the appearance falls between our reads of 26 October 18:34 UTC and 27 October 06:34 UTC, on CISA's dates 26 October; the three clocks are kept apart in [the 27 October checkpoint](checkpoints/2026-10-27.md). An entry is CISA's classification of a flaw as known to be exploited, by criteria the feed does not state (observation of a classification); it is not a count of attacks, a measure of compromise or a statement about any named system, and the catalogue gives no evidence of its own. Reads continue under the brief, for CVE-2026-115560 and for any revision of the two entries.

## Answer, at the first read

CISA's feed, catalogue version 2026.10.23 with a release time of 23 October 17:00:09 UTC and 1,755 entries, lists none of CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619 or CVE-2026-115560. That is CISA's classification as of that release, two days after the fixes and after Amazon's and Cloudflare's public claims of exploitation attempts (LH028); it says nothing about whether exploitation occurred. Later reads will show whether and when any is added, by CISA's `dateAdded` and by our reads.

## Limits

An agency's catalogue applies its own evidence threshold, which is not published per entry, and the feed states no criteria at all; the brief's description of an entry as CISA's judgement of "reliable evidence" is not supported by any retained source (notes/R-0035-php-cisa-evidence.md, M1), so this record uses the feed's own terms; its absence of an entry is not a negative finding. The feed's release time is CISA's clock.
