### Oracle (rule panel; two bodies unreadable, class carried)

Both `www.oracle.com` routes answered HTTP 403 with 3,987 bytes: an HTML page titled "Technical Difficulties" that says "This site is experiencing a technical difficulty. We are aware of the issue and are working as quickly as possible to correct the problem", with an incident number. The two bodies differ only in that number. On 2 and 5 November both URLs answered 200 under the old User-Agent. The Oracle Linux pages, on `linux.oracle.com`, answered 200 under the new one, with text unchanged.

Why `www.oracle.com` answered this way is **unknown**. The changed User-Agent is one possible cause, as are an outage or an edge rule unrelated to this reader; the body names none of these, and a status 403 with a "technical difficulty" message does not settle it. The robots gate let both URLs through, which under scripts/robots_gate.py means that host's robots.txt answered with a status below 500 and did not disallow them; which status it gave is not in the read log. Under the brief this is a refusal at two bodies, not an absence: the alert said on 5 November that it "will continue to be updated as additional product patches become available", so a revision between the reads would not be seen. **Oracle's class:** *affected, fixed for all listed*, carried from its statements as last read (the alert at Rev 2, 26 October, read on 5 November; the Oracle Linux errata of 21 and 22 October, read today).
