We have not seen AI activity on the internet keeping itself going or spreading, and nothing public we have read describes it happening outside a lab. What we can show is the ground it would have to cross: an ordinary software update [reached half its downloads within two days](../articles/where-software-updates-go.md) while software that pins its versions took weeks, and the public instruments that watch the internet [each see one piece of it, whose counts cannot be added up](../articles/what-we-can-see.md).

Our reading, not a measurement: if such a pattern began, copies on public registries would leave traces within hours, but one agent reading another's work and acting on it [would leave none we know of](../essays/what-the-watch-would-see.md), so we would probably learn of it late.
