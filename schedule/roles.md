# Focused scheduled roles

**In short.** Four focused sessions share one queue and the same scientific rules. A role selects responsibilities, not a new schedule or a new readiness model. Follow [runner.md](runner.md) for claiming, evidence, completion, recovery and reporting.

| Kickoff role | Queue selection | Meaningful checkpoint and handover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watch | `kind=observe`, and `study=log` for the PHP watch log | Keep each permitted attempt, its evidence and checkpoint; flag changed claims for Research. Finish the claimed attempt and log, leaving newly ready analysis for Research. No extra source reads to finish analysis. |
| Research | Remaining tasks, including scientific `plan`, `analyse`, `review`, `publish` and study close-outs | Work a bounded analysis/review/publication chain to a retained checkpoint. Keep unresolved fixes explicit. The named version needs all existing reviews and the final recheck before release. Leave discovery to Horizon and presentation to Editor. |
| Horizon | `study=horizon` | Follow [the sweep plan](../notes/horizon/plan.md): discover, evaluate with a fresh gate, commission allowed work by fixing its brief and queue rows. Finish the sweep and hand over collection to Watch, analysis to Research. No outcome collection under a discovery claim. |
| Editor | `kind=maintain` with `study=site`, plus the daily visit in runner step 7 | Follow [the editor's plan](../notes/editor/plan.md). Read all intervening handovers; refresh the presentation and digests, obtain the independent reader check, retain the visit note and verify the build. An editorial task cannot clear a scientific review or release a draft. |
| All (default) | Every eligible row | Preserve the existing end-to-end daily pass, including the editor's visit. Use for compatibility or rollback, without overlapping focused triggers. |

These rules are implemented in `roles.py` and require no queue migration. A study's closure decision or protocol amendment belongs to Research even when `kind=plan`; a horizon sweep is identified by its existing study field. Every active row belongs to exactly one focused role. A role does not alter dates, dependency checks, explicit blocks, stale ownership or the scientific meaning of a task. Checkpoint measurements that are inseparable from an observation remain part of that attempt; separate analytical checkpoint rows belong to Research.

Claim only the next task you will now do. End after its bounded checkpoint, or continue the ready chain within the role while there is useful context to retain. If nothing is eligible, Watch, Research and Horizon record that and stop. Editor still performs runner step 7 even with no ready maintenance rows, unless today's visit is already retained; only then does it stop. Do not fill an idle session with another role's work or invent a new promise. A handover is the existing row, its evidence reference and a short continuation in its note/run record, not a parallel backlog. A run record's `by` and `did` name the role; keep the existing public `run_id`, ledger format and attribution rules.

Research updates the record, current account, investigation, release record and required publication surfaces. Editor curates the front-page digests from those outcomes, using reviewed findings only; unreviewed material stays labelled in the Now box. Watch and Horizon record leads and observations without presenting them as findings. All roles retain AGENTS.md's scientific, safety and evidence requirements. No role interprets another agent's agreement as independent confirmation.

Rollout is separate: choose external trigger times, ensure Watch can meet all fixed observation windows, let Research follow its handovers, retain Horizon's Tuesday/Friday cadence and give Editor one daily slot after relevant builds. Verify push permissions, fresh-review and browser capability in the actual harness, keep run identities distinct and watch recovery warnings. Replace the old trigger when enabling focused ones; rollback switches back to the all-purpose kickoff without restoring an old queue or discarding newer evidence.
