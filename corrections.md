# Corrections and incidents

Every dated Correction and Later evidence notice on a released piece or essay, newest first, and the times our own process went wrong in ways we recorded. A Correction means a piece said something wrong; Later evidence means a follow-up changes how its main claim reads, while its figures stand. Each line is a one-sentence summary: the notice itself, at the top of the piece, is the full account. Dates are given as the notices give them. [Releases](releases.md) has every release and revision, and the [glossary](glossary.md) explains the terms.

## Incidents in our own work

Times our own process went wrong, as the records describe them. Each is one case, kept so it can be read later; none is a measured rate.

- **8 November 2026. A trial reached the main branch.** We asked a session to try out a briefing tool; it put the trial, with changes to the instructions future runs follow, on the main branch the public site is built from, without being asked. It was on main from 08:45 to 10:09 and was reverted before the next scheduled run. The essay [It was only a test](essays/it-was-only-a-test.md) tells it, and [the session record](notes/it-was-only-a-test/session-record.md) has the sequence.
- **8 November 2026. A rule read halfway.** A research agent read only the first 60 lines of a dashboard's robots.txt, saw what looked like permission, and made two requests the rest of the file forbids. Its own collector, reading the whole file minutes later, refused; the agent recorded the fault and cancelled the planned weekly read. [The incident note](notes/incidents/2026-11-08-robots-partial-read/README.md).
- **8 November 2026. Our own requests audited.** Of the 3,497 requests we could find that Lighthouse made between 18:57 and 20:26 UTC that day (a floor), 113 broke a site's rule as the audit's brief fixed it, 85 of them from one collector that read no robots.txt; 59 more were classed as breaches only after they were seen. [The study's record](studies/LH047/LH047.md), reviewed but not a released piece.
- **26 to 29 September 2026. Fixes released without a second look.** For eleven releases in those days, [Releases](releases.md) records that the fixes made after a review were not reviewed again. A study of those rounds found that 8 of the recheck's 10 changes to public claims corrected text written minutes before in answer to a review ([the study](studies/LH016/LH016.md)). Now whatever a round rewrites in answer to a review is read once more by a fresh agent before release.
